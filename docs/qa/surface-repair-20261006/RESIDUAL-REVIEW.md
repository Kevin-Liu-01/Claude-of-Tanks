## Bounded repair outcome

AMX-40, all three Leclerc-family IDs, both Chieftains and AbramsX now pass actual HIGH/LOW finite-stock regression checks. AMX-40 and Chieftain use convex stock bounded by their original eight corners, Leclerc closure floors move to 0.285 m beneath their retained 0.295 m outer roofs, and AbramsX orders only the terminal backing courses below the original 0.100 m local roof. No casting or whole-vehicle hull was convexified.

M48’s lower-glacis roof ended at 1.080 m below its 1.100 m floor; the bounded wedge now uses its original convex envelope while the casting remains unchanged. Object695’s skirt return narrowed to X1.600 past its X1.660 inner datum; the finite convex return keeps all eight source datums and its existing 90% vehicle scale. Hetman II’s thin shoulder crossed opposite roof/floor diagonals across changing inner deck seats; outward physical facets retain all ten stations and 37/56 mm inner/outer boundary depth. Both quality paths have zero retained primary-stock crossings for these three IDs.

The second-pass regression checks **52 actual HIGH/LOW parts** and rejects **seven independent historical malformed inputs**. First-hit comparisons on 5,766 rays per first-pass vehicle found Chieftain unchanged, and Leclerc changing only the 15 mm backing underside. AMX-40 and AbramsX intentionally change ray hits where the old crossed solids missed the near-side face; preserving their boundary datums must not be reported as byte-identical exterior geometry.
