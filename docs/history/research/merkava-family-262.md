## Source and comparison contract

- Raw inputs: `~/Downloads/Claude of Tanks Models/merkava-mk{1b,2b,2d,3b,3c,3d,4b}.zip`, each holding
  `source/<name>.zip` (the OBJ, its MTL and textures) and a `textures/` folder; listed with `unzip -l` before
  extraction into `~/cot-lanes/merkava-family-262/source-models/` (outside the repository; never staged). Hashes per
  mark in the mark packets.
- Format: Wavefront OBJ with MTL, game-extracted (War Thunder asset names: `vehicle#bone_turret`, `ex_armor_*`,
  `mg_twin`, texture names `merkava_mk_*_c_isr_camo_green`), per-group meshes with flattened hierarchy (no node pivots).
  158-219 groups, 217k-275k triangles. No licence or author record in the archives: owner-supplied, reference-only;
  no redistribution right is claimed. The playable path is first-party procedural.
- Limitations: no node transforms or pivots (the turret ring and the trunnion are measured or constructed, below);
  ownership flattened (turret armour, hatches and stowage are separate groups but carry no parent); the WT track is a
  thin strip. Nothing omitted at registration.
- Approved target: the supplied models (owner, 2026-10-27). The earlier oracles for these ids
  (`public/models/tanks/community/recovered/merkava{1b,2b,2d,3c,3d,4b}.glb`) are decimated prints of the same game
  assets: the 2B, 2D and 4B prints match the supplied bounds within 2 cm; the 1B, 3C and 3D prints carry guns 0.45 m,
  0.78 m and 0.75 m longer than the supplied models and no antennas. Their registration is superseded (new oracle
  revision; earlier ledger rows stay in git history).
- Conversion: `node tools/source-x-oracle.mjs --prepare=<obj> --recipe=<recipe> --report=<report>`, recipe
  `{"axes": ["x","y","z"], "scale": 1, "translation": [0,0,0]}`, raw hash pinned; writes
  `public/models/community-candidates/<id>_supplied_x_source.glb` (ignored, never staged). Canonical hashes per mark;
  registered in `tools/source-world-registration.mjs` (fused) and routed in `tools/procedural-fidelity.html` and
  `tools/visual-evaluator-page.html` with `qualityBar: 'exemplar'`, `fixedMount`, no component masks.
- Frame: identity. The sources are metres, +Y up, +Z the bow, ground at y 0 (track grousers to -0.02/-0.03), hull
  centred. +X is the vehicle's left: verified against the Latrun Mk 2's right-side photo
  (`mk2_latrun_side_right.jpg`, Bukvoed, CC BY 2.5): the tall tube at the turret's right rear and the exhaust
  louvre on the hull's right side are at -X in the Mk 2B source (tube x -1.04, louvre x -1.87); OBJ and recovered
  print agree on the side of every named sight.
- Datums: ground y 0; turret yaw centre = the plan centroid of the shell's base course at deck height 1.70 (the
  sources' deck); gun pivot = the trunnion in the source's gun mask at the nose slot (0.40-0.45 m behind the shell's
  nose course; the OBJ carries no pivot, uncertainty about ±0.2 m along the bore); bore axis and muzzle from the
  `gun_barrel` group.
- Component ownership: fused (whole-source comparisons only; no fabricated turret or gun masks).
- Camera/registration: the registered fidelity and evaluator paths (`certified-source-world`, shared cameras).
- Quality bar: `exemplar` (92 per registered view and geometry component).
- Published dimensions vs source: the specs' published figures (8.63 m gun forward for the Mk 1/2, 9.04 m for the
  Mk 3/4) disagree with the supplied models (muzzle 8.76 m ahead of the stern on the Mk 1/2 sources, 8.84 m on the
  Mk 3, 9.22 m on the Mk 4); the build follows the sources.

### Owner decisions (reported with release 262, not made here)

1. The Mk 1B, 2B and 2D specs fire 120 mm rounds; their gun is the 105 mm M64 (the source's barrel, muzzle radius
   0.078 m against the 120 mm guns' 0.085 m). Ammunition is a stat change.
2. Spec dimensions (`overallLengthM` and friends) are the published figures, not the supplied models'.
3. The Mk 1B's spec registers two removable turret packages; the supplied Mk 1B carries seven turret `ex_armor` plates,
   so the build binds them (the earlier rebuild's "no turret armour" reading of the 1B came from the published
   history, and is superseded by the supplied model).
4. The supplied Mk 3B has no playable id.
5. `merkavaFamilySource.ts` commits the fit's measurements (part centres, sizes, attitudes, two-station loft sections,
   turret stations, barrel radii), not source triangles; the owner may prefer them kept out of the repository.

## Construction work order

- Gross form: three chassis lofted from their measured courses (`MK12_HULL`, `MK3_HULL`, `MK4_HULL`: deck, the
  Mk 1-3's grille step and full-width brow at z 2.9-3.1, the glacis, nose, belly and raked stern; sponsons over the
  track lane between their measured undersides and tops), each mark's turret shell lofted through 0.05 m stations of
  its source's turret mesh (16 levels above the deck, smoothed by a running median, posts standing clear of the shell
  drawn as their own cylinders), the bustle basket and ball-and-chain curtain, every source detail group as a box,
  cylinder or two-station loft at its measured centre, size and attitude.
- Running gear: the sources' six road-wheel stations a side, the return rollers at their own heights and radii, the
  sprocket and idler; the Israel MBT dished wheel face (`merkavaDishedFaceLayers`). The track keeps the fleet's thicker
  standard (FSP-04, owner 2026-09-25): a measured source departure of about 3-5 cm below the sources' thin strip.
- Gun: one solid of revolution through the source's measured barrel radii (tube, sleeve sections and clamps,
  evacuator, muzzle); the mantlet lofted through its measured stations; the 12.7 mm M2 scaled to the source's back
  plate and muzzle (`FLEET_M2`: the fleet fitting's own measured span) with its cradle and side ammunition box; only
  the barrel recoils.
- Primitives: `sectionSolid`, `revolveZ`, `KIT`, `FITTINGS.pintleMG` (roof MAGs as working riding-cradle stations,
  the over-gun M2), `FITTINGS.antennaWhip`, `markSmokeTube`, `markVehicleNightLens`.
- Cost (CPU, instance-expanded triangles, HIGH / LOW): the six builds 101k-110k / 88k-95k against the retired pile's
  110k-126k / 96k-110k. The HIGH-to-LOW reduction stays weak (13 %, as before): recorded, not solved here.
- Material roles: hull and turret paint for the shells and armour, painted stowage, dark mechanism steel for vanes,
  hinges and cradle caps, night-lens glass on the lantern faces, sight glass, rubber flaps.
- Negative spaces: the basket's open frame and curtain, the running gear, the louvre's recess.
- Armour: source turret `ex_armor` plates are each mark's two removable packages (`merkava_<seed>_turret_era_R/L`,
  `_R` on +X); the cast shell is the permanent backing (receipt: stripping both leaves the shell's vertices and extent
  unchanged; reset restores). Hull `ex_armor_body` plates are external armour (skirt panels on the track-guard owners).
