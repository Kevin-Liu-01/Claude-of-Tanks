The principal hull/turret/barrel arrangement is recognizable and close. The strongest remaining difference is the wheel face: source quarters and side views show deep annular dishes with raised hubs and distinct rim stock, while the candidate presents shallow near-flat discs. Correct the source-measured radial/axial stock rather than moving the wheel centers to conceal it.

In `close-front` and `close-roof`, the source gun-root boot has a rounded layered transition into the barrel; the candidate root reads as a box-like rectangular receiver. The source cupola carries prominent hinge/bracket structure on its lid, while the candidate reads as a largely plain circular cover. Sight receivers also simplify the source slit/edge geometry into generic rectangular optic boxes. These differences remain visible above the running gear and cannot be explained by the shared track shader.

The rear has the correct two-bank concept, but the candidate louvers are fewer/coarser in appearance than the source's tightly spaced blades. Source front/rear towing fixtures and bow corner lamp/cover detail are weak or unresolved at their source locations. Do not infer that the dark source lower hull is absent material: a candidate lower plate looking brighter is not by itself proof of extra geometry. Measure any proposed lower-hull correction in the unchanged source frame first.

The source central sight/sensor between the banks has a conspicuously rounded upper housing, while the candidate reads as a small box-like head. The source roof smoke assembly has a substantial staggered, inclined multirow cluster with supports; the candidate's lower, simpler row does not reproduce that arrangement (`close-roof`, `close-front`, `front`). Correct measured count, positions, inclination and brackets, not merely the number of tube ends.

Source rear access/port/tow details, canister latch stock and launcher support junctions remain simplified or weakly resolved. Wheel faces are also shallower and more generic than the source. Heavy camouflage can obscure small panels, so the review does not claim every low-contrast panel is absent. No unsupported roof weapon or open missile bore is requested.
