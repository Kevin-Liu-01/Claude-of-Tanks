# The Russian 152 mm line (release 269): batch contract

## Request and rulings

- Owner, 2026-11-04: "NEW RUSSIAN 152 MM TANK LINE make a new russian tank called object 195 (image 1) because it has a
  152 mm gun i think will rlly make gameplay very fun adn itneresting, as well as a t14 armata with a 152 mm variannt (image
  2), Object 477 "Molot" (image 3 and 4), and object 292 for a full line of 152 mm variant tanks".
- Main, 2026-11-04 (DECISIONS "2026-11-04 Russian 152 mm line"): four ids, each a whole real vehicle on its real base: the
  Object 292 on the T-80 builders, the Molot and the Object 195 on their own hulls, the T-14 152 on the t14X shell with
  t14_armata byte-identical; each 152 mm gun its own sound row and booming banks.
- Lane finding, sent to Main 2026-11-04: image 2 (the Gur Khan hangar photograph) is the first Object 195 prototype (Gur
  Khan, 17 Oct 2018, gurkhan.blogspot.com/2018/10/95.html; bmpd, 18 Oct 2018, bmpd.livejournal.com/3381493.html); image 1
  is a 2008 conjectural fan drawing (Commons Ob'yekt_195.jpg). The Object 195 follows the prototype photographs.
- Main, 2026-11-04, on the lane's fairness numbers: the Molot IX and the T-14 152 X with no lever; the Object 292 VII with
  its levers in the spec (900 damage, 2,500 HP, turret front x1.35 for its steel weight blocks, 9 s in place of a rate the
  prototype never got); the Object 195 X with the smallest trim that gets its mean to 0.65 or less with two thirds of its
  pairs fair, HP, damage, reload or rounds before any armour cut.
- Owner, 2026-11-04 (in the lane): "use our proper tank generation procedure and dont use the old t80 hull only use modern
  new hulls like the nnew t80 x hulll". The Object 292 moves off the legacy T-80 builders (profiles/t80.ts back to its base
  bytes) onto the T-80U X hull and casting (profiles/t80uX.ts) by recipe reuse; the line follows this handbook: a run packet
  per id, this contract, the photo packet and its fixture, the round audits and the release gate.
