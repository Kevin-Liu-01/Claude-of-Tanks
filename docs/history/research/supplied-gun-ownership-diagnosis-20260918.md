The minimum repair is to route those existing primitives to `gunMount` / `gunMountDark` before bucket merging. Do not rename the whole barrel, add a hidden proxy, alter source dimensions, move the pivot, or transfer all cannon geometry out of recoil. Source static geometry alone does not prove a fine-grained mechanism, so the boundary is the authored receiving housing versus its explicit tube/collar stock; any ambiguous part needs its source component/shape checked before migration.

Material preservation needs an explicit narrow step. The existing `gun` bucket uses `P.mats.barrel` (StandardMaterial, roughness 0.8, normal scale 0.5), while `gunMount` defaults to `P.mats.hull` (PhysicalMaterial, roughness 0.88, normal scale 1.3). Reassign only these migrated gunMount meshes to the existing barrel material in their profile post-assembly callback, preserving the exact original painted material. Both buckets already share the same camo-UV and dirt-bake inputs. Existing dark receiver stock moved from always-retained `gunDark` to detail-LOD `gunMountDark` also needs its original always-retained visibility preserved; moving only that real mesh out of its LOD wrapper preserves the prior range contract. Do not change global bucket defaults.

Required regression evidence before accepting a repair:

1. HIGH/LOW neutral expanded world-triangle multisets and per-vertex normal/UV/color plus material signatures match the frozen original; document any unavoidable renderer-only difference explicitly.
2. A real gunMount mesh contains the measured housing stock, pitches across the full published depression/elevation limits and remains seated to turret and barrel. Use complete filled native first-hit/stock witnesses, not only the whole turret AABB.
3. During the existing recoil hold, a receiver witness stays fixed in gun-local space while a true barrel witness translates; after recovery both return. Include yaw and legal pitch extremes without changing mesh/triangle counts or geometry identities.
4. TML optical recesses remain actual first complete-scene hits through yaw, pitch and recoil; the receiver and lenses no longer move with the tube. Existing physical muzzle negatives stay intact.
5. AFT retains all eight distinct launch anchors, sealed rectangular covers, no generated cannon furniture and zero recoil across repeated indexed launches; the actual support beams pitch with the launcher.
6. Existing full gun-articulation and recoil suites, focused optical/attachment tests, selected HIGH/LOW budgets and final render identity comparison must pass. Regenerate affected owner-dependent anatomy/fills through the parent before final captures if required.
