## Finite findings for this revision

BMP's revised broad hull, aft wings/access notch and gun proportions are coherent. Its remaining substantial defects are physical, not inferred from camouflage:

1. Two source forward-turret roof assemblies are omitted. Complete-source FrontSide rays at X.426/Z−.6584 hit `Object_14` at Y2.734179974; native HIGH/LOW hit the bare turret at2.607892154, a126.288 mm stock deficit. At X.138/Z−.412 the source hits2.652763488 and native bare turret2.577438217, a75.325 mm deficit. Source component1 is the larger cylindrical receiver;163/169 and related trim belong to the adjacent small assembly. The source component census also records the actual first-occluding trim185; do not add only a plain cylinder without the measured receiver.
2. The rear source door has a shaped port (`Object_11` component12, bounds X−.1399..+.2241,Y1.3498..1.6441,Z−3.3274..−3.2428). The native profile still calls the generic `rearRamp` helper, with a flat slab, bottom hinges and handle. At the port center the source first hit is Z−3.314441219; native is the broad plane−3.325000048. A native plane extending beyond a source recessed center does not represent the port contour. Measure and retain the source door's actual relief instead of adding a floating circular decoration.
3. Bow protective guards exist. The early image-only suspicion of missing guards is withdrawn: both native top rays hit real `hullDetail`. They stand43.160 and40.391 mm higher than the corresponding complete-source first hits at(.537,3.452)/(−.621,3.563). This is a bounded source-station discrepancy; the original raised guard form should be measured before changing it. Actual Garage light also proves visible wheel-dish relief, so no new wheel-shape omission is claimed.

Griffin's lower sight pocket and all four aperture shapes remain visible. The rear door/round port initially appeared weak in camouflage, but actual complete-scene first hits prove their exposed physical stock. The door lower ray agrees within.122 mm. The native port center projects24.050 mm farther rearward than the source center, the upper hinge25.683 mm; these local depth/fine-relief simplifications are recorded and do not amount to a missing assembly or an independently established below9 exterior defect. No extra port, replacement body or shading change is recommended from camouflage alone.

The localized HIGH/LOW proofs and exact component selectors are in `.qa-dev/tank-run/kurganets-rear-door/independent-native-rays.json` and `independent-bmp-griffin-study.json`; the harnesses beside them only read source/native stock.
