The census measures every regional kit the way the owner reads Verdant's buildings: element completeness at three
reading distances, in one vocabulary (the 10-12 B1 study, `~/cot-launch-state/2026-10-12/docs/horizons-buildings/`).
Each builder is built at seeds 11, 29 and 47 on its own map with the shared detail modules instrumented
(`tools/building-detail-census-loader.mjs`); a kit is scored over its dwelling-class builders and those it names as its houses (`ArchitectureStyle.dwellings`; all of them where it has none).
`src/world/buildingDetail.selftest.mjs` holds every kit and map to `src/world/buildingDetail.baseline.json`: a kit may rise, never drop.
