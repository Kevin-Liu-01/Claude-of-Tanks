## Primary factual controls

- The Bundeswehr's Marder page establishes the six-road-wheel course, 20 mm
  MK 20, MG3A1, smoke system, MELLS and the 6.88 × 3.38 × 3.23 m envelope.
- The Bundeswehr's Puma page establishes the 7.6 × 4.0 × 3.6 m protected
  configuration, 30 mm MK30-2/ABM, coaxial MG4A1, modular reactive armor,
  rear cage and six-road-wheel stance.
- U.S. Army Bradley documentation establishes the 25 mm M242, 7.62 mm M240C,
  dual TOW launcher, smoke banks and M2A3/M3A3 crew-role distinction.
- Rosoboronexport material establishes the BMP-3's paired 100 mm 2A70 / 30 mm
  2A72 plant plus three PKTM machine guns, and the BMPT-72's twin 30 mm 2A42
  plant and guided missile boxes.

## Authored identity targets

- **BMP-3 (ROK):** low amphibious six-wheel hull, broad low turntable,
  parallel 100/30 mm gun plant, PKT cue, smoke banks and compact roof weapon.
- **Ukrainian M2A3:** Bradley slab hull and offset TOW turret with full flank
  reactive tiles, field stowage, Ukrainian radio cadence and shielded MG.
- **BMPT Terminator 2:** T-72-derived hull with a completely replaced narrow
  unmanned station, twin 2A42 barrels, four supported Ataka tubes and NSVT.
- **Upiór:** uninterrupted heavy modular flanks, low faceted turret, raised
  electro-optical/missile head and a compact protected machine-gun station.
- **Marder 1A3:** long wedge hull, small offset two-man turret, 20 mm cannon,
  MELLS tube, thermal head, MG3 and horizontal passive appliqué rails.
- **M3A3 Bradley:** cavalry-recce Bradley with the canonical 25 mm/TOW plant,
  elevated command optics, extra missile stowage and dense bustle equipment.
- **SPz Puma:** low robotic RCT30 silhouette, deep Level-C flank modules,
  reactive bow course, elevated optics, coax cue and supported roof weapon.

Every turret fitting is yaw-owned and visibly seated. Hull armor remains clear
of the road wheels and the front/rear end transitions. The two BMP-derived
vehicles retain the donor's closed belly and its shallow, hidden 3 cm inner
shoe-envelope contact rather than deleting hull volume to satisfy a synthetic
corridor mask. Every vehicle retains exactly one animated detailed shoe course.
