# Type 100 — 2026-09-15

Owner (2026-09-15, parade photograph): "make a chinese type 100, that is a super detailed mix
of the puma and cv90mk4, just check the image and make it look like that." Packet:
`docs/references/tanks/type100.md`. Builder: `src/vehicles/profiles/type100.ts`.

## Design notes

- Hull frame: lower tub (x ±1.15, y 0.38–1.24), three nose planes as `slopedPlate`s (an
  8-corner `orientedSlab` whose inner face sits one plate thickness behind the outer face
  along the plate's inward normal), a crowned `polyMultiLoft` monocoque from the sponson
  floor (y 1.24) to the roof (y 1.80) with cut plan corners, deck grille and louvres, exhaust
  cowl, driver's hatch and periscopes, bow handrail, lamp clusters, tow cable, stern bins.
- Running gear: `KIT.buildRunningGear` — six r 0.34 wheels, rear sprocket, forward idler,
  three return rollers, 0.58 m `compact-ifv` track with a covered top run; skirt carrier wall
  + seven doors a side (leading door chamfered over the idler), dark face plates, bolts,
  rubber lip, three flank cameras a side.
- Turret (pivot hull (0, 1.80, −0.40)): faceted citadel lofted to y 0.78, two creased cheek
  modules a side, boxy mantlet, 105 mm `KIT.buildGun` (4.95 m, sleeve, evacuator, collar),
  commander's hatch with five periscopes, gunner's sight, the sensor mast (top 1.98 m above
  the ring), twin quadruple launcher pods (tubes translated inside the pod frame, then
  pitched −0.18 and yawed ±0.30 with the add transform), four LWRs, compact `openYokeRws`,
  two whips, bustle rack and sill. `P.topY` = roof + 1.20.
- Finish: spec `visual.scheme 'digital'` sand palette plus `sig_type100` signature preset
  (`camoPolicy.ts`, `main.ts` label, both i18n catalogs) so the garage defaults to the parade
  finish instead of the Type 99A service scheme.
