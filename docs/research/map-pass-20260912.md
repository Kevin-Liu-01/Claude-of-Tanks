## Road pass — distance-field roads, reference relief, crisp shadows (2026-09-12, evening)

Owner review after the restoration commits: "all our map textures look too
flat, for example the roads are so flat and everything looks so undetailed",
and the 30 August build "looks better while not being all glitchy and flashy
with the fuzzy looking shadows". Side-by-side with the 1049e4e player view
(`$SP/ref-gameplay/player_view.png` vs `head-gameplay-r9`) the road was one
uniform light-tan band and the ground had lost its near relief.

### What the shader diff showed (GLSL-only diff, 175 lines)

| system | 1049e4e | before this pass | cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| near soil octave | `dn * 0.85 * dNear` | `dn * 0.22 * openNear` | pothole fix flattened everything |
| clod octave | `dn2 * 0.75 * dNear2` | `dn2 * 0.18 * nearG` | same |
| road core | local albedo × tint, gravel 42 % near | deep-mip (+7) packed dirt, 25 % local | "oval stain" fix |
| rut wear | 0.55 dirt / 0.30 paved, far ×0.9 | 0.10 / 0.22, far ×0.45 | 2 m-texel Gaussian lanes read as beads |
| landform dapple | on roads | off roads | pothole fix |

The root cause of the bead/oval artifacts was the mask: `texSize(512)` over
1024 m is a 2 m texel, and two 0.55 m Gaussian lanes at ±1.55 m cannot be
stored in it. Every later "fix" removed detail instead of fixing the encoding.

### Change

- **Mask G byte = road centreline distance field** (`px[j+1] = 1 - d/12`).
  Bilinear filtering of a distance ramp is exact away from the centre kink, so
  `splatCompute` now evaluates analytically per pixel: the carriageway edge
  (`roadHalf = 3.85 ± noise gauge`), the twin wheel lanes
  (`lane = exp(-((d-1.55)·uLaneK)²)`, σ 0.42 m on the 2 m mask) and the crown.
  On a coarse 4 m (mobile) mask `uLaneK` is 0 and the lanes collapse to one
  bead-free compaction plateau (`1 - smoothstep(2.6, 3.6, d)`), because inside
  half a texel of the centre bilinear filtering can only overestimate.
- **Road surface**: packed-dirt palette from a smoothed mip (+4, was +7) mixed
  30 % with the local ground; clamped zero-mean gravel grain (rock-tile
  luminance high-pass, −16 %…+20 %) under 48 m; lane wear 0.34 dirt / 0.26
  paved with the ×0.9 far boost; lanes run 14 % less rough (damp compaction);
  +5 % dusty crown; lane relief from the analytic lane slope along the field
  gradient (0.14); along-lane tyre streaks (±8 %) from an anisotropic noise
  sample oriented by the field gradient; half the landform dapple stays on
  the carriageway.
- **Open ground relief**: first octave 0.70 (cap was 0.25), clod octave 0.60
  (cap 0.25), both still excluded from the carriageway.
- **Shadows**: Ultra mid cascade back to 4096 (`[4096, 4096, 4096, 2048]`),
  Medium range back to 520 m; the far-partition ellipsoid crown proxies are
  removed (1049e4e cast nothing beyond ~260 m; the ellipsoids read as soft
  blobs under every distant crown). Near canopy proxies are unchanged.
- Terrain program key `world-terrain-splat-v30`; one new scalar uniform
  `uLaneK`; three new lexical fetches (two rock luminance taps, one streak).

## Temporal anti-aliasing (2026-09-13, small hours)

Owner: "fix shadow flashing from trees and other objects, that's a
persistent issue" and "as beautiful as triple-A games while maintaining
extremely high performance". Two headless meters were built first
(`.qa-dev/shadow-flicker.mjs`: consecutive in-page frames, one-frame
luminance blips while driving or turning; `.qa-dev/jitter-sensitivity.mjs`:
the fraction of pixels that flip when the camera shifts by exactly one
pixel). Findings on Verdant, High preset: forcing every cascade to refresh
each frame, disabling AO, disabling shadows and hiding all alpha-tested
foliage each changed the driving blip rate by less than a tenth; the whole
frame is sub-pixel unstable (28.6 % of pixels flip under a one-pixel shift;
the 1049e4e reference 32.5 %). Git history shows three earlier "shadow
flash" fixes chasing the same symptom (AO boil, PCF phase, proxy
self-shadow). Nothing left in the scheduler was flashing; the image itself
was aliasing, which reads as flashing wherever shadow dapple, blades and
relief meet.

`src/engine/temporalAA.ts` (new): a Halton(2,3) sub-pixel jitter applied to
the projection for the scene render and restored afterwards, a resolve pass
after the late fx and before bloom (the accumulated HDR frame feeds bloom
and the grade) that reprojects the previous frame through the resolved
depth, clips history to the current 3×3 neighbourhood (variance clipping,
γ 1.0, so moving tanks and smoke do not ghost), blends with luminance
weighting (no HDR sparkle), weights history 0.9 (0.82 while the camera
moves fast) and rejects it only off-screen or on a large linear-depth
disagreement (a first non-linear-depth test quantised in the half-float
history and rejected almost everything). One resolve plus one copy at the
internal resolution. Presets: Ultra, High and Medium on; Low and mobile
off; the published AA state reads `taa+smaa-high+fsr1` (so timing pairs
against pre-TAA baselines fail the state guard by design).

Measured on Verdant, High: one-pixel-shift instability 28.6 % → 14.2 %,
consecutive-frame instability at rest 20 % (broken first pass) → 3.4 %,
driving blips 2.46 % → 1.84 %, turning 0.16 % → 0.39 % (the jitter's own
change; the reference measures 0.33 %). Headless frame rate unchanged
within noise (35–45 fps on the shared Mac).
