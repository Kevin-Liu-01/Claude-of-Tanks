improved significantly without slipping on performance, transition speed or
latency. The 1049e4e presentation restore (horizon rows, water, roots, belts)
landed on 2026-09-11; this pass reads the 30 establishing shots captured after
it and closes the most visible gaps that are configuration-level.

## Evidence read

Establishing shots for all 30 maps (`tools/map-environment-audit.mjs --shots
--establishing-only`, desktop tier). Findings:

- Coastal, Fjord, Urban outskirts, Saltwind, Airfield, Desert: bare foreground
  lawn/sand between the camera and the settlement; no scrub, few lone trees,
  few rocks. Saltwind read as generic green pasture, not limestone terraces.

  alpine range shading is per-fragment by design (r3–r8 in `horizon.ts`) and is
  left alone here.
- Verdant, Autumn, Reservoir, Frontier, Monsoon, Delta: already read well.

## Addendum — horizon r9 (alpine-style ranges)

The pale flat triangles on the far ranges of Nordhavn Fjord and Glacier Pass
were two things: per-vertex forest/snow/rock bands interpolating across 250 m
wall triangles, and the treeline crown groves rendered in the (paler) baked
face colour. `horizon.ts` r9 moves the treeline, crest snow and forest stands
into the alpine fragment pass (same map bands, three plane fetches, no
branches), softens the vertex bake to match, and tints the crowns by the
authored forest/base ratio. Paired establishing shots before/after are in the
scratch report; the receipts (`horizonResources` shader contract, mesa/redrock/
titan/autumn receipts, `mapQuality`) stay green. The remaining sawtooth
outline of far groves comes from the comb atlas alpha edge and is a later item.

## Addendum — Glacier Pass roads (2026-09-12, later)

The establishing shot still showed the alpine roads as 10 m black-brown
slashes across the snowfield. Two causes, both authored: the road tint
(`[0.65, 0.69, 0.72]`) darkened an already dark frozen-mud dirt layer, and
the splat's bare road shoulder (`shoulder` in the dirt weight `fD`) stripped
the snow from the whole corridor, so the tint alone moved the road colour by
under 10 % between 1.06 and 1.52.

- `terrain.ts` gains an authored `shoulderDirt` scale (`uShoulderDirt`,
  default 1, clamped 0..1) applied to the shoulder term alone; the max()
  competition between ambient wear, shoulder and town wear is unchanged and
  every other map keeps its full bare shoulder.
- Glacier Pass authors `roadTint: [1.30, 1.46, 1.70], shoulderDirt: 0.30`:
  the roads now read as packed grey snow with darker edges between white
  verges (r5 shot), still legible from the air.
- Frosthollow was shot as a control and already reads as snowed-over lanes
  (its dark-slush tint stays); Whiteout inherits Frosthollow's splat.
- Receipts: the authenticated leaves module carries the alpine line; the
  worn-dirt receipt executes the actual `fD` with the new uniform (810 cases,
  legacy default, clamp, scope = alpine only); the material-ownership policy
  pin and its uniform list are restated with this note.
