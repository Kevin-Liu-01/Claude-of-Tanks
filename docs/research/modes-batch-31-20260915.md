## Critical damage switch

`MatchRuleset.criticalDamage` (true everywhere, false in Turbo Ball) reaches the combat state
as `modeCriticalDamage` through `applyRulesetToCombat`. In `src/sim/damage.ts`
`rollModuleDamage` and `rollCrewHit` consume their chance draw and return when the switch is
off, so no module loses hit points, no state changes, no fire roll runs and no crew member is
knocked out — hull damage and the replay RNG order (pen, dmg, chance) are unchanged. The mode
card and pre-battle chips gain the line "no module, crew or fire damage"
(`rules.line.noCriticalDamage`). Receipts: matchRuleset (turbo flags and line order), combat
(module ray: 3 draws, engine keeps 160 hp, no event, hull −250; crew ray: gunner stays),
matchModes, authoritativeMatch, i18n.

## Revive countdown instead of the spectate/garage bar

Death in a respawning mode used to run the killcam death view and then hand the camera to
the ally chase, whose spectator bar carries the "Garage" button; the 3–6 s revive then
cancelled it. `spectate.maybeStart()` now returns when `game.ruleset.respawnS` is set. The HUD
shows a `cot-revive` card ("BACK IN n") started by the player's `tank:destroyed` event or, as a
fallback, by the frame-observed destroyed state (debug kills never emit the event), and hides it
on `mode:respawn`, on a new ruleset (`setPreBattleRules`) and on battle end. Probe:
`.qa-dev/death-ui-probe.mjs` (untracked): Turbo Ball death shows "Back in 3 → 2 → 1", the body
never enters `cot-spectating`, no end screen.
