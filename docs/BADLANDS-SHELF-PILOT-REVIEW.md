# Redrock shelf pilot: held, superseded by canyon direction

The first Badlands pilot changed three secondary tactical ridges into unequal
shelves. Focused geometry/support tests, all-map/all-mode spawn and route tests,
TypeScript, and direct public Vite/strip passed. The matched native packet
`badlands-relief-native-r1.kKI4wF` completed four original daytime views with
exact camera replay, stable source, clean page/GL state and complete cleanup.

Actual visual review rejects this as insufficient: dominant foreground terrain,
the tall west cliff and round distant peaks remain unchanged. Secondary shoulder
changes do not give the map a convincing regional identity. No collision shards,
minimaps, marketing pictures or release were generated from this pilot.

Kevin clarified that **Redrock should be a canyon**, not scattered tablelands.
The next design must coordinate a lower traversable valley floor, tall irregular
red-rock flanks, branching side ravines and a canyon-aligned backdrop. Maintain
viable roads and objectives, but do not preserve the generic mesa-noise landscape
as the dominant shape merely to minimize source changes.

The pilot's clean tests do not constitute art acceptance or performance/memory
certification. The evidence remains unchanged; this local checkpoint is not
approved for main.
