# AMX-30B2 roof equipment — September 30

The owner requested a cupola, machine guns, roof equipment and decorations for the AMX-30B2 after its turret was rebuilt around the AMX-30B casting. The B2 roof now has a raised commander cupola with ten vision blocks and a sight hood, a separate loader hatch, two complete supported MAG-style machine guns, a protected forward sight, smoke banks, antenna feet and whips, and strapped service stowage. These two roof guns are decorative; this change does not add another automatic weapon to the vehicle.

Structural coamings contribute to the turret's hittable armor. Roof fittings belong to turret yaw and remain independent of main-gun elevation. The old overlapping B2 crown/spine/bustle additions are removed where the new casting already supplies the shell. B2 cheek armor seats are measured against the actual replacement casting. The preceding AMX-family hull-waist closure is retained.

## Commander-side correction

The owner's follow-up caught a mirrored station arrangement. The cupola had been placed at positive local X while the adopted AMX-30B casting and source roof place the commander at negative local X. The complete commander station now follows the donor center `[-0.553, -0.520]` in turret-local X/Z; the loader hatch uses `[0.552, -0.439]`. This is the left side in a head-on game view. The attached commander machine gun, bearing, binocular hood and gunner sight move with that station; the loader's gun, hatch and periscopes move to the opposite side. The commander's bearing foot overlaps structural cupola stock.

The regression measures actual upper/lower roof surfaces at both stations and asserts the two machine-gun positions, alongside the contact and yaw/pitch checks. Explicit local coordinates avoid camera-facing or historical prose left/right ambiguity. Fresh front, close roof and aimed garage views were inspected after the correction.
