# Tank-generation history

These are tank-generation history records: source studies, source-X rebuilds,
opening and caliper studies, independent reviews of source fits, second-wave
and recovery inventories, and tank repairs that were built or reviewed against
an outside comparison model. They were moved here from `docs/history/research/`
on 2026-11-12 under the owner rule that outside 3D models are used only as
comparison bases in tank generation. Their text is kept as written; only links
to their new paths were updated. The registration recipes in
`docs/history/research/second-wave-registrations/` stay where they are because
generation tools read them by path.
