## Authored identity targets

- T-72M1 Jaguar: low six-wheel T-72M1 family stance, restrained Polish
  applique, compact bustle, Drawa/thermal station, and a shielded roof weapon.
- PT-91A Twardy: low T-72-derived hull, dense shallow ERAWA cassette cadence,
  asymmetric Polish optics, WKM-B station, and a supported bustle/service rack.
- PL-01: uninterrupted high stealth-skirt envelope, folded bow shoulders, a
  low diamond/faceted turret, paired electro-optical heads, and compact RWS.

All three retain one suspension-driven detailed track course. Exact band and
individual-shoe clearance, duplicate-course detection, bore, winding, turret
ownership, generated asset, and 45-view yaw evidence are release gates.
