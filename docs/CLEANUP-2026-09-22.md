# Repository cleanup — round 40 (2026-09-22)

Owner ask (2026-09-22): "we shouldn't have any hidden tanks, remove
unnecessary code from our game and repo. honestly turn this into a massive
refactor/cleanup pass that makes our repo much more clean."

This document is the phase-1 record: the inventory that was measured, what
phase 1 removed (with counts), the phase-2 plan with effort estimates and the
receipts each step moves, and the decisions only the owner can take. Phase 1
was scoped to removals that are provably safe without touching rendered
geometry, wheel/gear or muzzle code (owned by the wheel and bore agents this
round) and without re-pinning the shared fleet-digest receipts.

Base: `origin/main` 39f897a5d (deploy 60). Branch: `r40-cleanup`.

## 1. Hidden-tank inventory

Method: `src/vehicles/tankFactory.ts` (the eager facade every release tool
uses) was imported in Node and every saved id was crossed with the manifest,
tier and taxonomy tables, the generated anchors/anatomy/fill/marking-seat
groups, `public/icons/tank-assets.json`, `docs/references/tanks`, the geometry
ledger, `docs/geometry-gate/sealed.json` and `docs/FLEET-FREEZE-CURRENT.json`.
"Selectable in the Garage / gallery" was decided from code, not screenshots:
`src/main.ts` (lines 283, 1011, 1025, 2607), `src/gallery/gallery.ts:185` and
`src/game/studio.ts:3421` all read `VISIBLE_TANK_IDS`, which
`src/vehicles/specs.ts` binds to `PRODUCTION_TANK_IDS` unless
`developmentFleetEnabled()` sees `import.meta.env.DEV === true` together with
`VITE_COT_DEV_FLEET_KEY` (rosterPolicy.ts) — that is, only a local Vite dev
server. "Scored by the standard check" means `tools/tank-standard-check.mjs`,
`tank-release-plan.mjs` and `procedural-fidelity.mjs` accept the id, which
they decide with `validateSelectedIds(ids, Object.keys(FLEET_GROUP_BY_ID))`.

Totals after phase 1: **228 saved records** (was 231), **202** in the release
/ anatomy roster (`ALL_TANK_IDS`), **192 production-visible**, **34 hidden
development models**, **2 reference placeholders** (`recon_tank`, `q_heavy`).
Two mechanisms hide a saved record:

- **historical archive** — `isRetiredHistoricalTank()` (rosterPolicy.ts): a
  `ww2`-era spec not in `RETAINED_WW2_IDS`, or a Cold War candidate not in
  `RETAINED_COLD_WAR_IDS`, is spliced out of `TANK_IDS`/`ALL_TANK_IDS` by
  `finalizeFirstPartyRoster()`; it is not in the release roster, gets no
  anatomy/marking-seat receipts and no release gate;
- **production curation** — `PRODUCTION_HIDDEN_TANK_IDS`: the record stays in
  `ALL_TANK_IDS` (release gates, anatomy receipts, icons) but is filtered out
  of `PRODUCTION_TANK_IDS`, so no Garage card, bot seat or wreck cast.

## 4. Structural smells (report only) and the phase-2 plan

### 4.1 Machine-generated stage wrappers in `tankFactoryCore.ts`

`createTankOwnedSteps` and its neighbours are threaded through 91 closures of

Receipts that read the file's text and would move: `tools/battle-geometry-sharing.browser.selftest.mjs`
(SHA-256 fingerprint of the file), `src/vehicles/eraWholeFitReuse.selftest.mjs`
