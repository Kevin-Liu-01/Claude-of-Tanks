## Identity cues

- Sloped 60° glacis with driver hatch + bow MG ball; sides slope inward
  above the tracks (sponson overhang), full-length flat fenders.
- Cast 1.6 m-ring turret: rounded cheeks, gunner/loader roof hatches, rear
  mid-left commander cupola with split lid, twin dome ventilators.
- External stowage: twin cylindrical fuel drums on the right hull, spare
  track on the glacis, saw/tow cable, rear twin exhaust pipes.
- Christie wheels with big stamped lightening holes ('holes' wheel style),
  rear sprocket with rollers, front idler smaller.
