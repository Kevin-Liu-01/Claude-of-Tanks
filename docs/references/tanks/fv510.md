# FV510 Warrior (`fv510`) — reference packet

Exact variant: FV510 Warrior section vehicle, 30 mm L21A1 RARDEN in the two-man steel
turret.

## Corroborated real dimensions
- Length 6.3 m (hull = overall; the RARDEN does not clear the nose); width 3.03 m;
  height 2.8 m to the turret roof.
  Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FV510_Warrior ,
  https://www.militaryfactory.com/armor/detail.php?armor_id=27 ,
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RARDEN
- Gun: 30 mm L21A1 RARDEN — thin bare 2.43 m barrel (3.15 m overall), NO evacuator, NO
  sleeve, distinctive slotted muzzle; mounted high in the turret face, usually carried
  with a few degrees of elevation.
- Running gear: 6 road wheels per side, FRONT drive sprocket, raised rear idler, top run
  behind aluminium skirts/appliqué strakes.
- Distinctive identity: tall troop hull with a long shallow glacis and near-vertical
  ribbed flanks (horizontal appliqué strakes), rear power door, big boxy two-man turret
  set forward-center with flat sloped face, twin sight heads, two whip antennas and the
  tall L-shaped exhaust/NBC stack on the right rear quarter.

## Local GLB oracle (recovered drop, articulated)
Width-normalized reference (scale ×1.071): hull z ±2.83 (5.66 — proportionally shorter
than real; the GLB's width bound is its protruding WING MIRRORS, whose tips define
spec-width normalization — its body is only ~88 % of 3.03 m, so a mirror-less procedural
renders ~13 % oversized everywhere; matching mirrors were added), hull top 2.30, turret
x −1.36..+1.47 with sight/mast gear reaching y 3.9; RARDEN pivot at (−0.55, 2.0, 1.29) —
offset LEFT of center; barrel never passes the nose (gun overhang legitimately empty on
both sides → gun component 100 for matching geometry).

**ORACLE DEFECT (turret component):** the GLB's `Turret` node (TurretMesh, post-yaw
z −0.78..+2.64, 3.4 m long) fuses the entire upper BOW/glacis plating with the turret,
and its `Gun` node is a 2 cm sliver. The fidelity "turret" mask therefore includes hull
bow mass no honest rotating turret can cover — turret component is structurally capped
near ~36 (an assembly/followers fix in src/vehicles/userdrops5.js, outside UK-family
ownership).

## Procedural gaps identified (baseline 72.3: H80 T34 G100 R72)
- Procedural hull too long (6.34 vs 5.66) and far too low (top 1.82 vs 2.30).
- Turret much too small: ±0.82 wide × 0.93 tall vs oracle ±1.4 × ~1.2 plus masts.
- No exhaust stack / sight heads; skirt band and 6-wheel line sat too low (tracks R72).
