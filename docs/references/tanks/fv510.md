# FV510 Warrior (`fv510`) — reference packet

Exact variant: FV510 Warrior section vehicle, 30 mm L21A1 RARDEN in the two-man steel
turret.

## Corroborated real dimensions
- Length 6.3 m (hull = overall; the RARDEN does not clear the nose); width 3.03 m;
  height 2.8 m to the turret roof.
  Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FV510_Warrior ,
  https://www.militaryfactory.com/armor/detail.php?armor_id=27 ,
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RARDEN
- Gun: 30 mm L21A1 RARDEN — thin bare 2.43 m barrel (3.15 m overall), NO evacuator, NO
  sleeve, distinctive slotted muzzle; mounted high in the turret face, usually carried
  with a few degrees of elevation.
- Running gear: 6 road wheels per side, FRONT drive sprocket, raised rear idler, top run
  behind aluminium skirts/appliqué strakes.
- Distinctive identity: tall troop hull with a long shallow glacis and near-vertical
  ribbed flanks (horizontal appliqué strakes), rear power door, big boxy two-man turret
  set forward-center with flat sloped face, twin sight heads, two whip antennas and the
  tall L-shaped exhaust/NBC stack on the right rear quarter.
