# M113A3 (`m113a3`) — reference packet

## Current status (release 269, 2026-11-03): a new US M113

The owner, 2026-11-03: "make a USA m113 variant, that plays similar to the ares apc". The US Army's M113A3 (from 1987):
tier II in the United States tree's special lane, the tier the Ares APC holds in the UK tree's. Built as the 'a3' mark of
the Australia line's M113 builder (`src/vehicles/profiles/m113.ts`, `buildM113A3`; datums `m113Datums.ts`, the commander's
cupola 1.80 m behind the nose). Spec `usaM113Specs.ts`: a clone of the Ares APC (`ares_apc`), so it plays as the Ares does:
the same three 12.7 mm belts under their US names (M962 SLAP-T, M20 API-T, M33 ball), the same fire control and turret
traverse, on the M113's aluminium envelope (`m113Combat.ts`, shared with the Australian marks; the gun shield's 10 mm
steel), 12.3 t on the RISE powerpack's 275 hp, 66 km/h, crew 2 (the driver front left, the commander in the cupola:
`internalLayoutRegistry.ts` m113OneManTurret). Photo packet `usa-m113a3-20261103.json`; fixture
`profiles/australia.selftest.mjs`. Coat: the US Army's CARC woodland three-tone (forest green under field drab and black).
