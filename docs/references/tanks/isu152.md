# ISU-152 (`isu152`)

**Exact variant modeled:** ISU-152 (Object 241), 1944 production on the IS-2
chassis, 152.4 mm ML-20S gun-howitzer. Late-war fit with external fuel tanks
on the rear sponsons; no AA DShK (mid-production roof).

## Identity cues

- Casemate: full-width, low and long; front plate leans back ~30°, side
  plates ~15°, flat roof, slightly sloped rear. The mantlet is OFFSET RIGHT
  of the hull centerline (bolted onto the right — tank-afv/tanknutdave).
- Gun mount: two-part cast mount — fixed bolted ring on the plate + moving
  rounded ball shield; above the 152 mm tube rides the ML-20S's huge
  cylindrical recoil/recuperator sleeve (the "pig snout" stack).
- Hull front: pointed two-plate bow below the casemate; tow hooks both
  sides, headlight left, spare track links on the glacis.
- Running gear: 6 small twin steel wheels low on the hull, 3 return rollers,
  rear drive; front mud flaps rise over the idlers.
- Signature equipment: two cylindrical external fuel tanks per side on the
  rear sponson brackets, fender stowage boxes, saw/tool on the fender, two
  round roof hatch domes + panorama hatch, three periscope pods.
