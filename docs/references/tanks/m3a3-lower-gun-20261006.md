# M3A3 lower gun seat — 2026-10-06

The owner requested a lower M3A3 gun because the raised mantlet left a large
opening beneath it. This follow-up supersedes the raised-trunnion position in
the October 4 roof packet. It follows the broad base-shell audit published in
deployment 197; that audit covered 160 IDs at HIGH and LOW, not just the M1A3
and Qilin. See [the audit report](../../qa/base-shell-audit-20261005/README.md).

## Change

The complete M242/M240 mounting is 100 mm lower, at turret-local
`[-0.06, 0.392, 0.78]`. Fixed bearings and their welded saddles share the new
axis. A finite lower sill joins the turret cheeks and compartment floor; a
rounded pitching receiver shoe keys into the mantlet. The gun passages stay
open, and the barrel retains its independent recoil.

Full −9° depression remains available across the front and sides. The aft
hatch requires a mechanical stop: −9° through 150° absolute turret yaw,
transitioning to −5° at 160° and remaining there to 180°. This uses the
existing shared simulation/Gallery policy. Lowering without that stop causes
76.3 mm of barrel penetration into the rear hatch at yaw 163.55°.

The M3 hull and running gear, Ukrainian M2A3, and M6 geometry remain as in the
deployed base-shell repair. The old preservation fixture described the bow
before its triangulation repair; its replacement was measured from the
untouched `3d0c3c50e` production source clone and independently compared with
this candidate, rather than accepting candidate hashes as their own baseline.
