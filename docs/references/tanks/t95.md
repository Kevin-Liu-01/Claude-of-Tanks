# T95 (152 mm) (`t95`): run packet

The US T95 superheavy tank destroyer (the T28 Super Heavy Tank), the 1945 pilot in fighting trim with both outer
track units fitted, carrying a **fictional 152 mm gun** with the figures of the US Navy 6"/47 Mark 16.

## Current status

- Updated: 2026-11-05 (lane t95-270).
- Owner request (verbatim): "also use our generation procedure to make the t95 and its 152mm cannon. its model is inn my
  model ffolder". Asked which T95, the owner chose "American T95, 152 mm gun". Ruling (the owner's decision log, 2026-11-05):
  "American T95 with a 152 mm gun". Main accepted the datum (widthM 4.37, hullLengthM 7.62) and the 6"/47
  gun figures on 2026-11-05.
- ID and name: `t95`, "T95 (152 mm)" (short "T95").
- Implementation: first-party procedural build `src/vehicles/profiles/t95.ts` (own loader group `t95`), spec
  `src/vehicles/t95Specs.ts`.
- Qualification: in progress (see the evidence matrix). Publication: none beyond the lane branch `item/t95-270`.
- Worktree: `~/cot-lanes/t95-270`, branch `item/t95-270` from `f98c38c63c`.

## History

A `t95` existed before: the T95 "Doomturtle" community playable (2026-07-28), rebuilt in August against a World of
Tanks print (§5.317), retired with the hidden fleet on 2026-09-23 (`351f0d3dc7`) and its builder deleted on 2026-09-25
(`3227e83a51`). This is a new build from a new procedure; nothing of the old builder is reused. The old packet stays
in history (`git show 351f0d3dc7^:docs/references/tanks/t95.md`).

## Construction work order (as built)

- Gross form: one closed cast loft (`sectionSolid`, 12 stations) for the turtle-back upper hull and the casemate: the
  roof at 2.02 m, the 57-degree sides from the roof edge (x 1.02) to the skirt tops over the outer units, the bow
  rounding in plan and section from z -1.0 to the face at z 0.30. The lower hull tub between the inner units (belly
  0.45 m), the undercut lower bow plate, the track-unit covers forward of the casemate (inner edge 0.78 m, flat at
  1.20 m, noses sloping to z 1.90).
- Running gear: four units on 1.21 m and 1.83 m centres, 0.52 m tracks, eight 0.52 m road wheels a unit at the print's
  stations (z 0.67 … -4.465, four HVSS bogies), rear sprocket (z -4.93), front idler (z 1.17), three return rollers;
  spacer walls between the units; outer skirts with the print's bevelled foot.
- Gun: the hemispherical ball (0.58 m) on the gun axis (y 1.39, z 0.42) is the yaw and pitch pivot (`gunMount`), the
  tube tapering 0.168 → 0.142 m outside with a step, the double-baffle brake, a bore of 76 mm radius; muzzle on the
  print's at z 5.46. Traverse 10.5 degrees each way (`gunArcDeg`), +19.5/-5 degrees.
- Roof: the commander's cupola rear right on its posts and the M49C ring with the .50 cal; the driver's cupola front left
  with periscopes; the gunner's sight hood; three whips. Deck: two grille panels; stern lights, pintle, tow eyes.
- Kit: headlights in guards on the cover noses, tow clevises, tow eyes on the lower bow, lifting eyes, pioneer tools,
  tow cables and jack, stowage bins on both slopes.
