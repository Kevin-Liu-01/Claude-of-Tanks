# T95 / T28 Super Heavy Tank (`t95`)

**Exact variant modeled:** T28 Super Heavy Tank (a.k.a. 105 mm Gun Motor
Carriage T95), 1945 pilot, with BOTH outer track units fitted (4-track
configuration) and the 105 mm T5E1.

## Identity cues

- One-piece cast "casemate hull": low rounded superstructure blending into a
  massive cast bow; the gun sits low center in a heavy cast rotor/collar with
  a big bolted ring.
- Roof: commander's cupola RIGHT-rear with M2 .50 cal on a ring, loader hatch
  left, 2–3 whip antennas, periscopes, lifting eyes.
- Four-track lower body: each side shows TWO track bands separated by the
  unit side plate; from front/rear the four ground contacts read clearly.
- Long tube with a slight step mid-length; travel lock on the bow.
- Stowage: tow cable + tools along the sponson tops, taillights on the tail.

KIT DEFECT FOUND (repair queue, workaround in profiles/casemate.js):
buildRunningGear OVERWRITES P.gear on every call and the factory only calls
update(0,0) on the LAST gear — the first unit's InstancedMeshes render at
identity (an origin blob to 0.4 m BELOW ground that poisoned heightM to

explicitly before the outer unit is built. NOTE: end wheels also raised to
y 0.40 (wrap bottom above botY) — wraps that dip below the ground run
degenerate the clamped loop.
