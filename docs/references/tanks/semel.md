# Semel (`semel`) — reference packet

The Olifant Mk 1's twin build in the South Africa tree (release 269): the packet, sources and gap table are in
[olifant_mk1.md](olifant_mk1.md). The Semel of 1974: 35 Centurion Mk 5s under the Skokiaan's Continental AVI-1790-8
petrol V12 (810 hp) and Allison CD-850-5, the 20-pdr and the Mk 5's side plates and six-tube dischargers kept (the
Skokiaan survivor R90587 at Tempe). Built as the Centurion family's `semel` mark; `paint_semel` ("SANDF Bands Desert": the SADF nutria under broad dark-earth and olive field bands; owner 2026-11-03: no plain coats).
