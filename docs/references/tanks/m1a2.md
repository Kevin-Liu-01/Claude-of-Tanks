## Notes
- Oracle hull (7.25) is shorter than the real 7.93 in the scoring frame
  because the asset's widest point (skirt armor) sets the normalization;
  targets above are what the lab scores.

## Outcome (final lab state)
Baseline 70.3 (H74 T33 G72 R84) -> ~79.5 (H89 T57-59 G76-80 R85), min view
~77. Remaining gap is concentrated in the turret component: the dannzjs
upper works are a dense irregular cluster (low doghouse, tall CITV at
z ~1.5, CROWS-LP mast, sponson boxes with tumblehome) that a primitive
reconstruction only approximates; the front proof view also keeps ~4 pts of
IoU loss from the asset's asymmetric hull (x -1.71..1.83) which a mirrored
procedural cannot reproduce without an intentional lateral offset.
