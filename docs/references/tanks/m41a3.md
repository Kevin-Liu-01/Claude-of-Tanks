## Construction

- Gross form: a low boxy hull with sloped glacis and fenders over big dual road wheels; a long turret whose cast nose
  narrows to the gun shield, flanks widest low and leaning in, a long bustle over the engine deck.
- The cast loft: pattonCast.ts's castRing (floor, walls to the widest point, superellipse shoulder), stations from the
  print; the nose ends in a moving cast gun shield (gun frame) so the gun elevates without the casting.
- Running gear: the Pattons' call (dished wheels, arms, no sprocket teeth, hub caps, return-roller brackets); the
  wheel face is the US Patton-era M60A3 donor for every mark (nationWheelSets exceptions).

## Combat data

- Published (Pansermuseet, armyvehicles.dk, US data): 23.5 t, 500 hp (DK-1: 25 t, 465 hp Cummins VTA-903T), 72 km/h,
  four crew, 76 mm M32; armour 25.4 mm at 60 degrees glacis, 31.75 mm lower, 25.4 mm sides and turret, 38 mm shield, 19 mm
  rear, 12.7 mm roofs (modernArmor on the print's datums).
- Rounds: no penetration figure is published in the fleet's form for the M32's M331A2 HVAP-T or for the AAI APFSDS-T the
  Danes bought; both sit at the fleet's tier I / II light-tank footing for the balance session.
