# T-90 (`t90`)

**Exact variant modeled:** T-90 obr. 1992 (base production, "Vladimir" pre-A) —
CAST turret from the T-72B lineage wearing the Kontakt-5 clamshell chevron,
the two round red OTShU-1-7 Shtora dazzlers flanking the 2A46M, NSVT-12.7
"Utyos" on the commander cupola, '90s-plain deck. Distinct from t90a (welded
family rebase, §5.13), t90m/t90sm (welded + bustle), t90ms (Tagil export).

## Identity cues

- CAST dome (the 1992 mark — the print corroborates), max chord ~mid-dome,
  K-5 clamshell: two big leaf banks per side meeting at a pointed tip at the
  gun (§5.29 tip law is this print's own read), K-5 roof panels on the crown.
- The two ROUND RED Shtora dazzlers beside the gun = THE T-90 cue.
- NSVT-12.7 on the right commander cupola (open pintle mount, tall on the
  real vehicle — see caps below).
- '90s-plain hull: glacis K-5 rows (two), skirt-front ERA course (3 panels),
  plain rubber skirts behind, rear transom rack row + split unditching log.
