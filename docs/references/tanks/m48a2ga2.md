# Kpz M48A2GA2 (`m48a2ga2`) — reference packet

## Current status (release 266, 2026-10-28): a new German M48

Owner, 2026-10-28: "also add the m48 super to germany as well as the kpfz m48 a2 g a2" (with photographs of the M48
Super and a Bundeswehr M48A2G). Built as the `m48a2ga2` mark of `src/vehicles/profiles/pattonCast.ts`
(`buildM48VariantCastSteps`, `M48_VARIANTS.m48a2ga2`) on the M48A5's measured hull and dome
([m48a5_patton.md](m48a5_patton.md)); spec `src/vehicles/m48VariantSpecs.ts`, a clone of the M48A5 sharing its package
(the L7A3 is the M68's twin; ledger entry `m48a2ga2`). Germany tree: a new M48 line from the Jagdpanzer E 100, the GA2
at I and the M48 Super after it at II. Implementation: DONE; qualification per the release check.

Exact vehicle: the Bundeswehr's **Kampfpanzer M48A2GA2**, one of 650 M48A2C rebuilt by Wegmann in 1978-80: the 105 mm
L7A3 in its thermal sleeve on a larger cast mantlet, a low cupola with the MG3, Wegmann smoke dischargers, the square
searchlight on the mantlet, the petrol AV-1790 kept.

### Differences from the M48A5 build

| Item | Source | Build |
|---|---|---|
| Gun | 105 mm L7A3, thermal sleeve and fume extractor about 40 % out, no muzzle device (TE, photos) | `m48SleevedL7`: on the M68's stations, the sleeve in clamped sections either side of the concentric extractor |
| Mantlet | a larger cast mantlet: a rounded box standing ahead of the turret, the gun through its collar (TE, photos) | a 1.12 x 0.80 x 0.56 m rounded box on the gun frame over the A5's shield, its collar and ports |
| Cupola | a low cylindrical cast collar tapering to its rim, the Leopard 1's hatch, eight periscopes, the MG3 on the rim (TE, photos) | `m48Ga2Cupola` |
| Loader | no pintle gun | the loader's hatch alone |
| Smoke | four 76 mm tubes in a row low on each turret cheek, raked up and forward (photos) | two four-tube Wegmann banks under the handrails |
| Searchlight | the square IR/white-light box on the mantlet's top left, its lens in line with the mantlet's front (photos) | 0.60 x 0.50 x 0.58 m on its short bracket on the gun frame, elevating with the gun |
| Running gear | three return rollers on the A2 hull, no skirts (photos) | `m48Gear` 'a2' |
| Engine | the petrol AV-1790 (825 hp) kept; no diesel air cleaners on the fenders (TE, placard) | the A5's cleaners replaced by low bins |
| Stowage | the bustle rack and its box (photos) | the A5's rack with a stowage box across it; not the A5 model's drum, tow cable or flank racks |
| Coat | Bundeswehr | the Bundeswehr service coat (camo stock `service_leo2a6m`) |
