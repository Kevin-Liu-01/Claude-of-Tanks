# T-80U (`t80u`)

**Exact variant modeled:** T-80U obr. 1985 (Soviet Army) — 125 mm 2A46M-1,
Kontakt-5 ERA on glacis + turret front arc, GTD-1250 turbine, NSVT "Utyos"
on the commander's cupola.

## Identity cues (what makes this vehicle unmistakable)

- Turret plan-form and roof layout: low cast dome with FULL shoulders wearing
  the Kontakt-5 CLAMSHELL — two big wedge courses forming a V around the
  frontal arc that reads nearly hull-wide from the front; commander cupola
  right with NSVT "Utyos" 12.7 on its AA ring (tall silhouette forward-right),
  gunner hatch left, 1G46 sight doghouse left of the gun.
- Mantlet/gun mount: small embrasure between the clamshell halves; 2A46M-1
  with sleeve segments + prominent evacuator just ahead of mid-tube.
- Hull front: 68° glacis with a full-width 3-course K-5 wedge raft, V splash
  board, driver periscope strip at the glacis top.
- Running gear + skirts: 6 dished rubber-tired wheels visibly SMALLER than
  T-72 wheels, rear sprocket; rubber skirt sheet with fabric seams, front
  panels armored.
- Signature equipment: wide flat TURBINE exhaust box centered on the rear
  plate (T-80 tell vs T-72/T-90 side exhaust); transverse OPVT snorkel and
  stowage boxes wrapping the turret rear; rear fuel drums; unditching log.
