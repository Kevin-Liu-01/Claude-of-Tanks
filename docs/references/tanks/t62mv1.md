# T-62MV-1 (`t62mv1`)

**Exact variant modeled:** T-62MV-1 — 1980s deep modernization of the T-62:
Kontakt-1 brick ERA on glacis/turret front (MV fit), V-55U engine, KTD-2
rangefinder over the mantlet, Volna FCS. 115 mm gun retained. NOT a T-62M
(passive BDD brow armor instead of ERA) and NOT a T-55.

## Identity cues (what makes this vehicle unmistakable)

- Turret plan-form and roof layout: small hemispherical "egg" cast dome
  (~2.7 m), loader hatch left with DShK ring, commander cupola right;
  K-1 bricks in arcs on the front half of the dome.
- Mantlet/gun mount: bare narrow cast throat; KTD-2 laser rangefinder box
  ON TOP of the gun root; Luna searchlight right of the gun.
- Hull front: K-1 brick raft on upper glacis (3–4 rows); splashboard chevron.
- Running gear + skirts: 5 spoked wheels — silhouette gap pattern is a prime
  T-62 tell; bare track top run (no rollers).
- Signature equipment: two 200 L fuel drums on rear brackets, unditching log,
  long OPVT snorkel stowed; smoke via engine (no launcher cluster on MV-1
  turret front-left, launchers sometimes rear-right).
