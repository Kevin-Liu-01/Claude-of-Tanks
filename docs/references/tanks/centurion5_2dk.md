Mk IVB, 34 km/h, four crew. The published 650 hp on 51 t sits at 0.55 of tier III's power-to-weight median, which the fleet
balance receipt refuses, so mobility, hull, armour (x1.12) and rounds follow the Mk 5/2's post-pass footing written in
the spec; the 1985 fire control gives a faster, truer lay (aim 1.75 s, accuracy 0.28).
