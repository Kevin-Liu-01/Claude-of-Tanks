# Challenger 1 Mk 1 (`challenger1_mk1`) — release 262: the 1983 delivery fit on the shared build

## Current status

- Fit (owner 2026-10-28: "CHALLENGER 1 MK. 1 AND MK2 NEED TO LOOK A LOT DIFERENT FROM MK 3", then "the turrets and
  decorations and equipmennt and optics and machine guns all need to be updated for all 3"). The Mk 1 is the
  delivery fit of 1983-85: its TOGS barbette stands on the right cheek with the port blanked by a bolted plate (the
  barbettes were mostly empty until retrofit), it carries no rear fuel drums (the Mk 2 introduced their quick-release
  fit), and its decor is a clean load: one canvas roll and the fender tools, never the fleet cargo. Shared with the other
  marks: the commander's L37A2 GPMG on the No. 32 cupola, the cupola's nine glazed episcopes and MG sight, the loader's
  periscope, and the right smoke bank on the barbette's face (`challenger1XSuppliedTurret.ts`). It keeps the BAOR coat.
- Added 2026-10-27 (lane item/vickers-cr1-262) as the sleek-turret mark; on 2026-10-28 the owner ruled the Mk 3's
  supplied-file turret the real one ("like the challenger 1 mk 3 that is currently there, that turret is what it is actually like, and we should just use the weird turrets we invented to make nnew tanks like action X variants or connqueror orr smthg"). The sleek turret became the fictional FV4030/6
  ([challenger_x.md](challenger_x.md)), and the Mk 1 rides the Mk 2's build: the Mk 3's supplied-file build without the
  Op Granby kit ([challenger1_mk2.md](challenger1_mk2.md)).
- Name: the Mk 1 is the one real Challenger 1 mark the fleet did not carry (109 built 1983–85, TOGS barbettes mostly
  empty until retrofit; en Wikipedia "Challenger 1", Haynes 2015 p. 110). The build draws the TOGS barbette (it is part
  of the supplied turret's body), as on the retrofitted Mk 1s.
- Combat row: the Mk 2's (`additionalFleetSpecs.ts`), tier VIII, the Challenger line's lane 0 off the Chieftain Mk 5.
