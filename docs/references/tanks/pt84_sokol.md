# PT-84 Sokół (`pt84_sokol`) — packet

The light hunter of the PT-84 family: a first-party Polish game concept on the T-84BM Oplot-M base, built by
`buildOplotBM(P, 'pls')` (profiles/oplotBM.ts); the base, the Krata language and the shared parts are in
[pt84_orzel.md](pt84_orzel.md). Release 262, tier IX, Poland; ahead of the Orzeł on the Twardy line. Owner 2026-10-28:
"i would like mroe based on that". Owner-authored design record: `docs/references/concepts/pt84-family-20261028.json`.

## What makes it its own tank

- Slab armour (the 262 visual bar's blind critics: its few framed patches read as token windows, and its glacis field
  was the Orzeł's and the Żbik's): ERAWA-2 under bolted cover plates on the glacis (two courses), the cheek boxes and the
  three forward skirt panels only; the rest of the skirt box plain, plain welded flank boxes with seams (no flank field).
- Its running gear in the open: no hinged rubber skirt behind the armoured forward panels, the road wheels and return
  rollers showing (round 4: without its mast it was the Oplot-M's side).
- One gun tower, low: a single ZSMU-1276 with its 12.7 mm WKM-B on the commander's side, on the roof race (no tower).
- Its signature: a telescopic sensor mast, stowed on its tapered armoured housing on the bustle, the optronic head (day
  and thermal windows, the rangefinder's port under a visor, side cheeks) 0.8 m over the roof (the critics read it raised,
  1.3 to 1.9 m, as a flagpole or a radar tower on an MBT).
- Shared with the family: Tellur banks, the round PCO panoramic head, Obra-3 heads, the rear stowage box and field
  basket, a work lamp on each cheek box, green-grey drums on the stern.
- Finish: Poland's factory coat, the PL-01 digital (`service_pl01`), number 271.

## Combat data (ukraine.ts)

- ERAWA-2 (0.20 / 440 mm) on the glacis and cheeks, the skirt sector from the third-from-front panel only (0.12 / 320); no
  flank ERA.
- The KBM2 120 mm, 6.1 s: K279 APFSDS (22), K280 HEAT-MP (12), K281 HE (12); no gun-launched missile. Its depression is
  the Oplot-M's measured curve (no framed fields aft).
- 49.0 t, 1,200 hp, 72 km/h, hp 2,550, traverse 46 deg/s; precision doctrine (the sniper facet), view 470 m.
- Station: the low WKM-B turns -221..3 degrees (blocked toward the mast astern and the panoramic head ahead).
