# T-80 / T-80B / T-80BV first-party family reference

## Authorship rule

The live family is repository-authored procedural geometry. Historical or
community GLBs are quarantined read-only comparison oracles only; they do not
provide meshes, vertices, materials, textures, rigs or animations to runtime.

## Standardized family order

1. `t80` — original production protection fit with the lightest planted cheek
   applique.
2. `t80b` — heavier frontal/brow armor and revised combat-station fit.
3. `t80bv` — Kontakt-1-equipped derivative with dense turret and leading-skirt
   coverage.
4. `t80u` — later distinct protected turret/chassis detail set, retained as
   its own existing first-party builder.

## Shared mechanical identity

- Low turbine hull and compact low pear/cast turret.
- Front free idler, six road-wheel pairs, five return rollers and rear final-
  drive sprocket.
- One continuous linked-shoe course with raised terminal transitions.
- 125 mm smoothbore with a narrow integrated cast mantlet throat.
- Turbine deck, supported rear fuel/service fittings and low skirts.

## Variant cues

- T-80: broad exposed casting with restrained planted cheek pads.
- T-80B: heavier brow/applique mass while retaining exposed cast shoulders.
- T-80BV: two irregular levels of planted Kontakt-1 plus falling side returns.
- Every variant retains unequal cupolas/periscopes, commander NSVT, Luna and
  gunner sight, 902B smoke banks, two antenna collars and supported rear
  bins/basket.
