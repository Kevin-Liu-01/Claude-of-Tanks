# T-62 family: `t62_1975` (T-62 obr. 1975) and `t62mv1` (T-62MV obr. 1985)

Lane t62-275. Owner, 2026-11-21: "update the t62mv-1 and t62 obr 1975 to actually look like their historical
counterparts." Photo-class round (no comparison oracle): the photos and the published dimensions govern.

One first-party builder, `src/vehicles/profiles/t62Family.ts`, draws both marks on the T-54/T-55 family construction
(`t54Family.ts`), stretched to the T-62's hull and dressed with its own turret, gun and running gear. Each mark carries
only its own kit. It replaces three earlier builds: the oracle-chased obr. 1975 chassis and turret in `russia.ts`, the
T-62MV's T-72B 1987 turret transplant (`t62Modernization.ts`), and the unused FBX-measured X build (`t62mv1X.ts`).
`docs/references/tanks/t62mv1.md` keeps the history of those builds.

## Spec

- `t62_1975`: dims 6.63 / 9.33 / 3.30 / 2.40 (the oracle silhouette fields retired with the oracle). Its gun record
  stays the Leopard 1A5-derived one the balance pass tunes; it still says 105 mm, +20/-9 degrees. **Owner question**:
  set it to the T-62's 115 mm and +16/-6 degrees (depression is a gameplay change).
- `t62mv1`: the T-62 frame copied from the obr. 1975 (turret plates, crew, modules, manual loader); the gun record it
  had (the transplant's ballistics with its balance peer's fire control, +14/-6) so tier balance does not move; Kontakt-1
  zones `glacis_era_L/R`, `skirt_era_L/R`, `turret_era_L/R` (`t62mv1XArmor.ts` seeds, replaced by the cassettes' marked
  faces in anatomy generation). **Owner question**: its 125 mm calibre label against the drawn 115 mm gun.
