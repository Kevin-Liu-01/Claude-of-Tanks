## Configuration

The Swedish Lvkv 9040 in winter (photos 01-02; the only photographs). Read from the photographs' geometry (the vehicle
faces right with its bow toward the camera, so they show its RIGHT side, not the left the registrar's note gave): the
early CV90 hull (seven road wheels) with the Lvkv turret; ONE 40 mm Bofors L/70 (the cradle's large round cover, a short
ribbed jacket, the long barrel and its flash eliminator, the v0 radar on the cover); the drum of the PS-95 search radar
standing raised on the turret's far side, rear left (behind the right-side sight heads, daylight under it); the turret's
sloped front, the commander's tall sight head; in the M90 splinter with the unit's white lozenge on the hull side. Photo
03 is a Strf 9040 hull for the chassis. Army Recognition places the radar "on the right side of the turret"; the
photographs' geometry (the drum's base hidden behind the near roof edge and the near-side sights, the drum seen past
them) puts it on the far, left side, which the registrar's "rear-left" also says: the build follows the photographs.
