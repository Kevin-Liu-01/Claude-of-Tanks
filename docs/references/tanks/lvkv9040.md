## Configuration

The Swedish Lvkv 9040 in winter (photos 01-02, left side; the only photographs): the early CV90 hull (seven road wheels)
with the Lvkv turret, ONE 40 mm Bofors L/70 (long barrel, perforated muzzle), the large drum of the search radar standing on
the turret roof rear-left, the turret's sloped front and the commander's sight, in the M90 splinter with the unit's white
lozenge on the hull side. Photo 03 is a Strf 9040 hull for the chassis. Published drawings and figures fill the turret's
other sides (builder S says what is inferred). Note: another lane (item/ifv-kf51-modern-267) changes the CV90's own
builders for release 268; compose on them, never copy them.
