# Marder 2 — independent roster slot `marder2`

On 2026-09-29 the owner selected Marder 2 as a new addition; the older Puma S1 upgrade is preserved as SPz Wotan.
The supplied Puma replica (`spz_puma_s1_x`) now carries the Puma S1 display name.
The Wotan retains its existing identifier and progression.

The Marder uses a new seven-wheel chassis, plain side skirts, broad side
ventilation, rear troop ramp and tall two-person TS503 turret. Its 50 mm Rh503
configuration has a rocking mask and canvas collar in a real cheek opening,
with a separate recoiling barrel and coax. It has no inherited Puma missile rack
or invented roof gun. Crew and module geometry follow the new arrangement.

The reference is Bojoe’s museum photograph (CC BY 3.0), linked in the shared
[photo packet](../photos/ifvs-20260925.json) and attribution register. Full outer
width, length and whip height are fixed photographic authoring estimates, not
measured 3D reference claims. Numerical 3D comparison remains unverified.

Penetration, damage, health and reload are game balance. Marder uses independent combat tuning; existing Puma S1, Kurganets and Borsuk derivatives retain their equipment and tuning.

`profiles/marder2.selftest.mjs` checks actual HIGH/LOW geometry, seven wheel
stations, crew, gun ownership and muzzle articulation, absence of missile racks,
and derivative independence across repeated registration.
