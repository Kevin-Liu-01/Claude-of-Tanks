### Law discoveries banked this round
1. **FRUSTUM-BOTTOM-STRIP CLIP CLASS:** a full-length side-band frustum
   whose bottom edge sits below the wrap crest inside the lane clips the
   wrap even when the fender above it is fixed — split the primitive at
   the ramp line and start the aft piece ABOVE the orbit crest on the
   same slope plane (silhouette-preserving split).
2. **AIMED-VS-FLUSH DRUM JUDGMENT:** the drum-class rx rule (−(π/2−rake))
   applies to plate-mounted rings/collars; AIMED equipment (headlights,
   MG barrels) keeps its aim vector and gets a riser bracket instead —
   deriving aim from plate normal is wrong on steep plates.
