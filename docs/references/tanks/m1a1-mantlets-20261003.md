## Construction

The fixed shell has a genuine opening between its existing asymmetric cheeks.
Its central receiver ends behind the trunnion. A camouflaged pitching shield
follows both cheek noses: 1.216 m wide in a 1.240 m channel, with 12 mm nominal
lateral seams, a 235 mm plan stagger, a raked front and beveled lower corners.
The short rear heel remains below the fixed roof and above the shell floor
through elevation. The existing turret bearing and its deliberate visible ring
remain unchanged.

The new helper `profiles/m1a1GunMount.ts` builds a rounded cross-shaft, a closed
three-section forged cover narrowing toward the tube, and a concentric fixed
barrel cradle. Painted retaining rings and a canvas seal have separate material
roles. The coax has its own finite boss and tube. HA and Ukrainian HA-derived
models retain the large gun-owned searchlight on a connected tapered support.
The mask and cradle pitch with the gun; only the barrel and thermal sleeves
recoil. Thermal sleeves now share the bore axis.

There is no new external source geometry. Existing turret datums and the
owner's appearance request are the target. Existing registered reference
comparisons remain applicable to the release tool; neither a missing source
nor an old failed comparison may be called a pass or silently waived.
