# M113AS4 (`m113as4`) — reference packet

## Current status (release 269, 2026-11-02): a new Australian M113

The stretched M113 of 2007: tier II in the Australia tree's cavalry line after the MRV, beside the Ares APC it shares its
12.7 mm and its footing with (capability 0.205 against the Ares's 0.208; provisional until the same-tier duels run after
the regen). Built as the 'as4' mark of the lane's M113 builder (`src/vehicles/profiles/m113.ts`, `buildM113AS4`; datums
`m113Datums.ts`): the M113A1 hull stretched 0.666 m with a sixth road wheel, the ceramic appliqué, the rear fuel tanks and
the Tenix one-man turret. Spec `australiaSpecs.ts`: a clone of the Ares APC (`ares_apc`), its armour re-enveloped on the
build's datums with the appliqué (70 mm glacis, 60 mm sides), 18 t (Army Recognition), the MTU 6V199 (the spec's 350 hp
has no figure in the notes **[?]**), the Tenix turret's 12.7 mm M2 as its main weapon, crew 2. Photo packet
`aus-m113-20261102.json`; fixture `profiles/australia.selftest.mjs` (six road wheels). Implementation: DONE;
qualification: the packet's envelope PASS, regeneration and critics pending.
