# T-90A (`t90a`)

**Exact variant modeled:** T-90A "Vladimir" (Russian Army, mid-2000s), cast
turret with Kontakt-5 wedge ERA, Shtora-1 IR dazzlers flanking the mantlet,
ESSA thermal sight. NOT the T-90M (welded turret) and NOT the obr.1992 (that
fit is `t72bu` here).

## Identity cues (what makes this vehicle unmistakable)

- Turret plan-form and roof layout: low, wide cast dome (~3.2 m across with
  K-5 packages), nearly flat crown; commander cupola right-rear with TKN-4S;
  cross-wind mast on roof rear; NSVT/Kord mount at the cupola.
- Mantlet/gun mount: narrow slit mantlet; two Shtora-1 OTShU-1-7 dazzler
  "red eyes" flanking the gun root; V-shaped K-5 wedges on both front cheeks.
- Hull front: two-piece K-5 glacis wedge rows over the upper glacis; splash
  board; driver hatch center.
- Running gear + skirts: 6 dished wheels, rubber side skirts with front
  rubber mudflaps; rear unditching log and transverse OPVT snorkel stowage.
- Signature equipment: 902B smoke tubes clustered on the left cheek; fuel
  drums on rear brackets common.
