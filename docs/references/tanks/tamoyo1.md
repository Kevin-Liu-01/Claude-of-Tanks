## Identity (Tanks Encyclopedia "MB-3 Tamoyo 1/3", after Bastos' "Bernardini MB-3 Tamoyo"; Bastos 2022 pp. 4-8)

New Bernardini designs, not M41 conversions, sharing the M41's concepts (torsion bars, the CD-500-3 on the Tamoyo 1).
Contract 06/84 CTEx; three Tamoyo 1 completed (TI-1 restored at the CIBld Alegrete, TI-2 at CTEx), one Tamoyo 2 (a
drivetrain test bed, out of this line), one Tamoyo 3 (Bernardini's export MBT; never Army-tested; now with a private
collector on an M41C powerpack). Cancelled 24 Jul 1991; the Army chose the Osório.

## Combat data

Welded steel (the Tamoyo 1: hull front 40 mm, sides 19 mm, turret front 40, shield 50, sides 25 mm); the Tamoyo 3's
composite and spaced turret front and front-hull cavity armour at a light MBT's footing (no published figure).
