# ZTZ-100 Prototype (`ztz100_prototype`)

## Family hull — 2026-10-27 (release 262)

Owner 2026-10-27 ("the ztz 100 is its prototype"): the testbed's hull and running gear are now the ZTZ-20 family base
at its earliest stage (`profiles/ztz20Family.ts` `ZTZ20_STAGES.ztz100_prototype`: bare glacis, upper skirt band only,
a closed stern bin, the flat testbed engine deck its missile turret's 1.425 platter clears at every yaw). The missile
turret of the 2026-09-19 owner decision is unchanged; its concept row was amended for the family hull envelope
(`missile-turrets-20260919.json` `amendments`). Packet: `docs/references/tanks/ztz20.md`.


The owner-directed Tier IX prototype preserves the original chassis and running
gear, with a new low armored turret carrying eight round 152 mm missile
canisters in two open four-cell pods. A short 35 mm cannon occupies the central
corridor. The service `ztz100_x` remains unchanged.

On 2026-10-21 the owner asked for launcher tubes "like" the Object 695 and the
TOS-1A. The armored box banks became pods in the Object 695 vocabulary, built
from the shared `profiles/missileCanisterStock.ts`: proud mouth collars with
dark bore liners around recessed sealed terminals, rear caps, clamp bands and
post-and-strap frames, with rails, an inner cradle plate on each trunnion
journal and a fixed elevation drive on each pitch pedestal. The eight cells,
loads and reload are unchanged. The tighter 0.30 m canister pitch moved the launch
mouths to x ±0.93/±1.23 and the lower row to y 0.12, and the top fell to 2.64 m.

This is an original game concept requested on 2026-09-19. Its HJ-P9 missile
names, armor, handling and weapon performance are authored game values. Three
crew sit in the hull; the turret is unmanned. Tandem and blast rounds share the
physical launcher cycle. The backup cannon has its own ammunition feed and
recoil mechanism. Missiles leave the corresponding actual pitching tube mouths
in both local and authoritative play.

The [design contract](../concepts/missile-turrets-20260919.json) replaces the
former full-vehicle preservation target for the turret only. The old preservation
export remains historical evidence, not a current comparison target or a
self-certification score. Native HIGH/LOW tests authenticate the unchanged hull
and running gear and exercise the new turret's actual stock, seats and aiming
range. The launcher's eight cells serve both missile ammunition types.
