# ZTZ-100 Prototype (`ztz100_prototype`)

The owner-directed Tier IX prototype preserves the original chassis and running
gear, with a new low armored turret carrying eight large missile canisters in
two four-cell banks. A short 35 mm cannon occupies the central corridor. The
service `ztz100_x` remains unchanged.

This is an original game concept requested on 2026-09-19. Its HJ-P9 missile
names, armor, handling and weapon performance are authored game values. Three
crew sit in the hull; the turret is unmanned. Tandem and blast rounds share the
physical launcher cycle. The backup cannon has its own ammunition feed and
recoil mechanism. Missiles leave the corresponding actual pitching tube mouths
in both local and authoritative play.

The [design contract](../concepts/missile-turrets-20260919.json) replaces the
former full-vehicle preservation target for the turret only. The old preservation
export remains historical evidence, not a current comparison target or a
self-certification score. Native HIGH/LOW tests authenticate the unchanged hull
and running gear and exercise the new turret's actual stock, seats and aiming
range. The launcher's eight cells serve both missile ammunition types.
