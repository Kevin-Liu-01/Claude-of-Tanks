## Sources

The historical T49 (Hunnicutt, *Sheridan: A History of the American Light Tank*, vol. 2, as summarised in the War Thunder
forum's "90 mm Gun Tank T49" thread): a 90 mm gun on two T41E1 light tanks, the M41's last prototype, with a T41E3
rangefinder; trials from 5 May 1954, not adopted. The 152 mm gun-launcher turret is the form the games give it; the
owner's ruling builds that form from the fleet's own M41 and M551.

## Combat data

elevation and turret crew seats; hit points at the M41 DK-1's tier II footing, 24.5 t for the larger turret.
