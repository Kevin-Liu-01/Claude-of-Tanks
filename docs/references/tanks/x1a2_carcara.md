## Identity (Tanks Encyclopedia "CCL X1 Pioneiro", "CCL X1A2 Carcará", after Bastos' "Brazilian Stuart")

- The X1 Pioneiro (MB-1): M3/M3A1 Stuarts rebuilt (Biselli hull extension, engine and transmission; Bernardini BT-90A1
  turret on the French H-90's layout and ring, VVSS suspension from the 18-ton M4 tractor), 52 + 1 prototype, 4º and 6º RCB
  1977-94. "X1A" is a misnomer for it.
- The X1A2 Carcará (MB-2/MB-2A): a new Brazilian hull (not a Stuart conversion), 24 built, the 6º RCB Alegrete only,
  1981-94; the only indigenous Brazilian tank in service.

## Datums

Published: the X1 5.04 m hull, 6.04 m over the gun, 2.4 m wide, 2.45 m, 17 t; the X1A2 6.06 m (Tanks Encyclopedia gives it
both as the hull and with the gun: taken as the hull, the profile's 0.86 m gun overhang beyond it), 2.6 m wide (the table
2.4), 2.45 m, 19 t. Stations from Brian Gaydos' profiles for Tanks Encyclopedia (Bernardini_X1A2ProdCamo.png, X1Rear1.png),
scaled on the hull (the X1A2's at 154.8 px/m): the front sprocket at 0.82 m, three bogies of two 0.53 m wheels, return
rollers on the bogies at 0.98 m, the rear idler raised at 0.66 m, an 18-degree glacis to the 1.74 m deck, the turret roof
at 2.51 m (published 2.45, recorded as a conflict), the cupola 0.13 m proud.

## Combat data

Tanks Encyclopedia: the X1 hull front 38 mm at 17 degrees over 44 mm, sides and rear 25 mm, the turret 28 mm all round, 15
mm roof and shield, 256 hp, 55 km/h, four crew; the X1A2 hull front 15 mm at 25 degrees over 35 + 28 mm, sides 28 mm, the
turret 28 mm, 280 hp, 60 km/h, three crew. The low-pressure 90 mm guns fire the Cascavel's rounds, HEAT first.
