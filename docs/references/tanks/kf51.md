## Identity cues

- Turret: big angular faceted shell — strongly sloped cheek panels sweeping
  into a wide flat roof, stepped front crown, full-width squared bustle with
  slat/stowage; SEOSS panoramic sight tower center roof; sensor/antenna mast
  farm at the bustle; drone bay hatches on the roof; 'Natter' RWS perch rear.
- Gun: the FGS 130 mm — visibly FATTER and longer than a 120 L/44; heavy
  angular thermal shroud sections, big blocky muzzle section.
- Hull: Leopard 2 hull with continuous flat-face skirt courses and hexagon
  camo panels; crease glacis, driver front-right.
