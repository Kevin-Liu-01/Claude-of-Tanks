# T-64BV1 (`t64bv1`)

**Exact variant modeled:** T-64BV (obr. 1985 fit) — T-64B with Kontakt-1
brick ERA on glacis, turret front arc and skirt leading edge; 2A46-2 125 mm
with 9K112 Kobra capability. Distinct from T-64A (no ERA) and from T-72
(different 6 small dual road wheels, 4 return rollers, sprocket teeth).

## Identity cues

- Turret: low cast dome (~2.9 m) with K-1 bricks in a chevron arc around the
  front 180°; coincidence-rangefinder-era smooth rear; gill-less.
- Mantlet/gun: narrow throat, K-1 sub-arrays flanking; searchlight above gun.
- Hull front: full K-1 raft on glacis (4 rows), V splash board.
- Running gear: the six SMALL wheels + exposed upper run with 4 rollers is
  the family tell vs T-72; thin steel skirts common on BV.
- Rear: two fuel drums on brackets, unditching log, long snorkel tube.
