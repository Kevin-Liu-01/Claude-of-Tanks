## Reference scope

[CIO's C2 page](https://idv-otomelara.com/ariete-c2/) documents a
1,500 hp engine, wider tracks, four return rollers per side, electric turret
controls and modern commander/gunner sights. The
[Italian Army's 2023 report](https://www.esercito.difesa.it/Rapporto-Esercito/Documents/2023/ok_RE23_A4_ITA.pdf)
describes additional protection kits and a belly anti-mine plate.
These primary sources establish equipment direction. Exact attachment shapes
and concealed mounting dimensions are authored construction estimates, not
surveyed factory drawings. Native HIGH/LOW images must establish their visible
coherence and attachment. The model is not a claim of exact C2 photogrammetry.

The hull and primary turret deliberately retain the owner's selected C1
source proportions at 1.12 scale. Thus the 4.0432 m full visual width is a
game-design target, not the real manufacturer's bare-fender width. The main
gun keeps a functional 120 mm bore. The existing loader machine gun and a
second roof weapon must have actual receiver, barrel, mount and seating stock.
No active protection system, guided missiles, or autoloader is invented.

## Construction and gameplay

The family frame defines all installed rig datums once. Structural additions
are closed finite side/cheek packs and belly stock; the same authored sections
generate their gameplay faces. Adjacent packs retain real gaps and their
double-faced surfaces share one protection-layer identity. Optics and roof
fittings remain equipment, not broad armor proxies.

Protection ratings, reload improvements, fire-control handling, weight and
survivability are explicitly gameplay choices. They must be tested against
the definitive C1 and existing Tier X vehicles rather than represented as
classified real-world data. The round types and penetration remain the C1's
manual 120 mm inventory; mobility and handling provide the progression. Its
measured gun-clearance curve preserves forward/side depression and raises the
barrel around the skirt shoulders and rear deck; these model-specific stops
are shared by live gameplay and presentation tools.

Qualification requires real geometry, attachment, track motion, bore, armor,
marking, anatomy and performance checks, an independent 14-view HIGH/LOW
review, and the complete release sequence. This packet is not itself a pass.
