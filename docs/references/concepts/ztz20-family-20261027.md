## What the vehicle is

The **ZTZ-20** is a first-party concept: a Chinese next-generation main battle tank of the early 2020s, the production
type of the programme whose first running testbed is the **ZTZ-100 Prototype**, whose pre-production vehicle is the
**ZTZ-100**, and whose turret testbed is the **ZTZ-20 Prototype**. The lineage reads ZTZ-100 Prototype → ZTZ-100 →
ZTZ-20 Prototype → ZTZ-20. None of the three claims to be a fielded vehicle or a documented programme: the names, stages and
dates are the game's design history, built to read consistently with the supplied oracle ("[OD]" marks that model an
original design too).

## Design rationale

- **Role.** A main battle tank sized for the southern hills, plateau roads and river crossings where the 50-plus-tonne
  ZTZ-99 family is heavy to move: about 45 t, 1,300 hp, 72 km/h.
- **Crew capsule, unmanned turret.** The three crew sit side by side in the hull front under two hatches; the turret
  carries the gun, its bustle autoloader and sensors only. That buys the low 2.30 m turret roof and the small frontal
  area the oracle shows (the internal layout is `ztz100HullCapsule`).
- **Why 105 mm.** A high-pressure 105 mm with a compact autoloader keeps the turret narrow and the weight down; the
  ZTZ-20's matured round (870 mm at the muzzle) closes most of the gap to the 125 mm tanks while the ZTZ-100 fires the
  earlier round (840 mm). The tube's measured radius (72 mm) and muzzle station (z 5.175) fit a 105 mm-class gun.
- **Protection.** Raked composite cheeks either side of a narrow gun housing (the arrowhead), flank pods over the
  turret sides, appliqué tiles on the long shallow glacis, three-band side skirts and a slat cage over the engine bay;
  active protection from the two launcher towers on the production turret. No ERA: the gameplay plates are permanent.
- **Development steps the stages show.** The missile testbed proved the hull, the capsule and the unmanned-turret
  controls; the ZTZ-100 brought the gun turret with its systems still blanked or in trials form; the ZTZ-20 Prototype
  tried the new high-pressure gun and its mount in the arrowhead turret with ballast standing in for the pods; the
  ZTZ-20 fitted the armour pods, the hard-kill system, the ATGM weapon station, the coax's cheek housing, the ribbed
  brake, the glacis tiles and the cage.
