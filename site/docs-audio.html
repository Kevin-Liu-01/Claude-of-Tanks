Did it go in?

No interface sound tells you a shot hit: you hear it at the target, late by the distance, and about half a second later your gunner calls it.

Your crew saysIt means

No penetration.Didn't go through.It hit but did not go through: aim for thinner armor.
Ricochet.Bounced.It glanced off an angled plate.
Miss.Short. Adjusting.Over.Nothing hit: correct your aim.
Good hit. Something's damaged.Critical hit.It went in and broke a module.
Ammo rack hit. He's cooking off.Hit his ammo. He's done.His ammunition is burning: he is about to blow.
Hit his engine.Engine hit. He's slowing.His engine is hit: he slows down or catches fire.

Target destroyed.Target down.Tank knocked out.Your shot finished him.

Fire command and mode calls

When your sight settles on a spotted enemy with the gun loaded, the commander gives the fire command and the gunner answers. The AC-130 crew and every mode have calls of their own.

Gunfire

Every weapon fires one of 24 report classes; the bigger the gun, the louder and longer its report.

  • Rifle-calibre machine gun118 dB
  • Heavy machine gun124 dB
  • 20 mm autocannon128 dB
  • 25 mm autocannon130 dB
  • 30 mm autocannon132 dB

  • A distant shot is seen before it is heard, and hills between you and it muffle it.
  • On Mars sound travels at 240 m/s and arrives 14 dB quieter and thinner.
  • On the Moon almost nothing carries (60 dB less): you hear your own tank through its hull.
  • In the sight your own gun sounds from inside the turret, and the world outside dulls.

Engines and tracks

  • Each hull carries its own powertrain's sound: gas turbines spool and whine, diesels shift and rumble.
  • Tracks change with the ground and your speed: earth, hard surface, mud, sand, snow and water.
  • An enemy drone buzzes where it flies and rises in pitch as it closes.

Sound settings

Settings › Sound has six sliders and two alarms; changes apply at once.

  • Master volume

  • Engine & mechanical volume

  • Gunfire & impacts volume

  • Ambience volume

  • Interface & music volume

  • Crew voices & alarms volume

  • Critical-damage heartbeat pulse

  • Concussion ringing

Every sound is a recording made for Claude of Tanks with ElevenLabs before release; nothing is generated while you play.
