Clear space and size

Keep clear space around the mark equal to half its height on every side.

Draw the vector mark at least 20 pixels tall. Below that size, use the pixel icons drawn for 16 and 32 pixels.

Use the paper mark on dark grounds and the ink mark on light grounds.
