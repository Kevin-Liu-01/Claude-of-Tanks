Stills and film

Take a still at any moment, or render the sequence to a film.

  • Capture PNG saves the frame you see.
  • Export film renders every frame offline with motion blur, drops none, and mixes in the battle's sound, as MP4 or WebM, whichever your browser encodes.
  • Live recording captures the screen in real time instead.
