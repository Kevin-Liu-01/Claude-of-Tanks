Battle roles

A role is how the tank plays: the bots drive it that way, and the Garage filters by it.

  • Breakthrough79

    Heavy armor and hit points: leads the push and absorbs fire.

  • Frontline42

    Balanced main-line tank: armor, mobility and firepower in proportion.

  • Flanker46

    Fast and agile: strikes the sides and relocates quickly.

  • Sniper25

    Accurate long-range gun: fights from cover at distance.

  • Scout15

    Spots for the team and avoids sustained fights.

  • Support33

    Autocannons, missiles or fire support that back the tanks.

Kits, smoke and the drone

Kits are unlimited; each waits out its cooldown, which starts only once the kit has done something.

  • Field Repair KitEvery damaged module back to working · 35 s cooldown
  • Large Repair KitRepairs every module, then 60 s of +50% self-repair · 60 s cooldown
  • Spare Track LinksBoth tracks back to working · 12 s cooldown
  • First Aid KitEvery injured crew member back · 45 s cooldown
  • Field Rations40 s: reload −5%, aim time −8% · 60 s cooldown
  • Fire ExtinguisherPuts out a fire · 25 s cooldown
  • Automatic Fire Suppression45 s: a fire that starts goes out at once · 60 s cooldown
  • Governor Override10 s: engine power +25%; may strain the engine · 45 s cooldown
  • Ready-Rack RestockRefills each round type by 30% of its stowage · 90 s cooldown
  • Smoke Grenade ReloadOne more smoke salvo, up to 3 · 40 s cooldown
  • Fresh Foliage60 s: +12% concealment while stationary · 60 s cooldown
  • APS Interceptor20 s: intercepts the next missile or HEAT round from the front · 45 s cooldown
  • IR Jammer12 s: missiles guided at you fly unguided · 40 s cooldown
  • Smoke

    A screen that hangs for 18 s; 28 s between launches.

  • Drone

    A 40 s battery, an 850 m link and 350 m spotting; 25 s before the next one after you lose it.

  • Roof gun

    Fires on its own at enemies and drones in reach; B switches it.
