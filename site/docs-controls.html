Hold or toggle

Settings › Accessibility can switch each of these between holding the key and pressing it once.

  • Fire gun
  • Handbrake
  • Gun hold (free aim)
  • Team rosters

Touch

On a phone or tablet the battle shows its own controls.

  • Left thumb: the joystick drives.
  • Swipe anywhere else to aim.
  • Tap Fire to shoot. Hold and drag to aim, release to fire; hold longer to keep firing. Drag into the cancel zone to hold the shot.
  • Scope enters the gunner's sight.
  • Pinch to step the sight's zoom.
  • Auto Aim locks onto the enemy nearest the reticle.
  • Rocket jumps or rights the tank, in modes that have a jump.

Watch mode

Watch a bots' battle from the Garage: Watch sits beside Deploy. Every seat is a bot's, every tank is visible and nothing is recorded. It works in 12 modes.

Drone

Fly anywhere over the battle. You open 70 m up at 14 m/s; the wheel steps the speed (4 / 8 / 14 / 24 / 40 / 64 m/s) and Shift boosts ×2.5.

  • W A S DFly
  • SPACE CClimb and descend
  • L-SHIFTBoost
  • WHEEL ↑ WHEEL ↓Step the speed

Ride along

Follow any tank from behind, or look through its gunner's sight at ×2 / ×4 / ×8.

  • FRide the tank in the middle of the view
  • Q ENext and previous tank
  • L-SHIFTGunner's sight
  • VBack to the drone

Take control

Take over the tank you ride and fight it yourself. If it dies, the drone takes you back after 3.5 s.

  • ENTERTake control, or hand it back

Your HUD

  • Settings › Gameplay › Edit HUD layout: move or hide each panel over a battlefield preview; Desktop, Phone portrait and Phone landscape each keep their own layout.

  • F10 switches to the minimal battle HUD: the reticle, range, zoom and ammunition stay, the other panels hide. Press it again to bring them back.

  • On touch screens, turn on Minimal battle HUD in Settings; a three-finger tap brings the full HUD back.
