02 Modern arsenal

Thirty machines.
Thirty new frames.

Fresh deterministic captures of the strongest current modern builds. Every image was staged on a live battlefield and photographed through the in-game Scene Studio—no reference model swaps, generative art, or offline beauty render.

03 Combat stack

One shot.
Every consequence.

The reticle, muzzle, shell, struck plate, module damage, event stream, killcam, and results report all trace back to the same resolved shot.

01 / Kinetics

Honest gun convergence

The bore tracks the center-screen world point one-to-one within real traverse, elevation, depression, and rotation limits. The gun marker exposes physical lag and obstruction.

02 / Armor

Plate-level resolution

Impact angle, normalization, ricochet, overmatch, spaced armor, composites, ERA, modules, crew, fuel, fire, and ammunition racks.

03 / Mobility

Terrain-shaped running gear

Support sampling, damped hull attitude, wheel contact, flexible track paths, measured scroll, crushable cover, and fixed-step movement.

04 / Visibility

Information is simulated

View range, concealment, foliage, firing bloom, movement bloom, and the 15 m rule determine what AI and network clients are allowed to know.

05 / World

Eight authored ecosystems

Height fields, material splats, roads, vegetation, structures, destructibles, collision, sky, fog, and minimaps are generated together.

06 / Device

Desktop depth, mobile reach

Adaptive resolution, shadows, post, textures, vegetation, and background work preserve the game rules while fitting the device.
