07Field manual — quick answers

the facts, straight
Q1Is Claude of Tanks free to play?Yes — completely free, in the browser tab you already have open. No install, no account, no payments.
Q2What do I need to play?Any modern desktop browser with WebGL2 (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). Recent phones and tablets work too, with touch controls on a reduced quality tier.
Q3How many tanks and maps are there?102 vehicles across tiers I–X — WWII to modern MBTs — and 8 battlefields across six biomes.
Q4What is the Scene Studio?The built-in staging rig (F8 in the garage, or /studio): place any vehicle on a live battlefield, pose it, set damage states, fire real effects, freeze time, capture up to 6144 px. Every gallery frame above came out of it.
Q5What engine does it run on?Pure Three.js on WebGL2 — no game-engine framework. Procedural terrain and vehicles, cascaded shadows, an HDR post chain, deterministic effects.
