01 // Current system

The game, not a promise.

Claude of Tanks is a browser-native armored combat game built directly on Three.js. The current runtime seals 121 first-party procedural playables, composes 16 authored battlefields, advances combat at a fixed 60 Hz, and supports local bots, persistent private/LAN rooms, and dedicated ranked authority.

01

Authority is explicit

Input requests actions. Movement, reloads, hits, damage, visibility, destruction, and results are resolved once by authority.

02

Presentation is measured

Three.js samples state and adapts visual cost without changing combat rate, map dimensions, ballistics, or armor.

03

Assets prove themselves

Live geometry, fingerprints, generated diagrams, deterministic screenshots, browser probes, and release gates stay connected.

Scope statementVehicle statistics and diagnostic overlays describe this game simulation. They are not real-world protection, engineering, or survivability assessments.

02 // Graphics + drivers

A direct WebGL presentation driver.

The engine creates and owns the Three.js renderer, color output, camera, lighting, sky, fog, post-processing, shadows, GPU timing, quality policy, warmup, and context recovery. Recent work fused the output and grade path, stabilized high-refresh presentation, preserved clarity through battle loading, and removed the moving-camera shadow phase changes that produced flashing.

Driver responsibilityCurrent mechanismFailure behavior
Image clarityDevice-capped pixel ratio, adaptive internal scale, subpixel AA, fused output/grade.Optional resolution and post cost adapt before gameplay.
ShadowsStable cascades, texel anchoring, bounded contrast, articulated tank shadow proxies.Quality tiers reduce cascade cost without altering visibility rules.
Shader readinessBoot and transition warmup of battle-critical material/pass variants.Countdown waits for required visual work instead of compiling on first fire.
GPU safetyCapability probes, framebuffer validation, context-loss recovery, black-frame rescue ladder.Optional shadows, environment, fog, and post can be stepped down.

Source owners: src/engine/renderer.js, post.js, lighting.js, deviceDiag.js, renderScalePolicy.js, and the shadow stability/refresh self-tests.

03 // Tank design

One specification; every visible and simulated consumer.

The live factory currently reports 121 first-party procedural battle playables, zero GLB-sourced playables, and seven isolated comparison candidates. A stable vehicle specification feeds garage cards, combat authority, bots, icons, anatomy diagrams, the Gallery, and Scene Studio.

specificationcanonical

Dimensions, mass, engine, movement, weapon, armor, modules, crew, labels, markings, nation, class, and tier.

procedural riglive geometry

Hull, turret, gun, recoil group, fittings, suspension, road wheels, flexible track path, shadow proxies, and LOD.

combat anatomyauthority

Authored plates, internal volumes, crew stations, muzzle and articulation anchors, hit logic, and damage state.

release receiptverification

Fresh hero/side/top assets, geometry fingerprints, anatomy diagrams, focused profile checks, and gate output.

Recent vehicle work added differentiated IFV statistics and sound/runtime behavior, visible recoil, magazine autoloaders, missiles and special actions, Swedish siege suspension, vehicle-specific fidelity profiles, and component-scoped geometry receipts.

04 // Combat + killcam

The shell begins at the bore and ends in one resolved story.

  1. The center reticle requests a finite world-space point.
  2. Authority bounds turret yaw, gun pitch, traverse rate, and dispersion.
  3. The actual articulated muzzle transform spawns AP, APCR, APFSDS, HEAT, or HE.
  4. Travel and gravity find the first eligible world or vehicle contact.
  5. Armor evaluates struck plate, angle, slope, normalization, ricochet, overmatch, spaced layers, ERA, and ammunition behavior.
  6. Damage mutates hit points, modules, crew, fire, repair, tracks, and destruction once.
  7. The HUD and X-ray killcam explain that completed result.
Killcam invariantThe X-ray view is a presentation of authority data. It cannot generate a second penetration or damage outcome.

05 // Worlds + physics

Sixteen tactical places, one quality floor.

Verdant Fields, Sirocco Wadi, Frosthollow, Steinburg, Saltmere Bay, Amberford, Tarkhan Steppe, Cinder Junction, Frontier Basin, Nordhavn Fjord, Jade River Delta, Redrock Divide, Monsoon Ridge, Glacier Pass, Obsidian Caldera, and Ironworks share the current world systems.

Each map combines authored routes and brawl/scout/support strongpoints with smoothed macro terrain, non-glossy material programs, atmosphere, textured structures, narrow collision, modern wreck families, detached remnants, pushable loose props, crushables, topple state, linked utility wires, vegetation, craters, and durable networked destruction. The original eight received the same backport pass as the expansion maps.

06 // HUD + UI

One visual language from garage to debrief.

The current interface includes the redesigned vehicle dossier and component workshop, improved map/camouflage previews, autoloader shell rack, stabilized aiming readouts, room chat, local lifetime statistics, responsive nation rails, debrief/rematch flow, settings/graphics/sound controls, and compact FPS/ping telemetry. Debug and shadow-tuning surfaces remain URL-gated instead of leaking into normal play.

07 // Multiplayer

Clients request. Authority decides. Rooms persist.

Client-owned

Presentation and requested intent.

  • Normalized drive and aim
  • Fire/ammo/equipment edges
  • Local movement prediction
  • Snapshot interpolation
  • HUD, audio, FX, reports
Trust boundary

Authority-owned

Every value-granting outcome.

  • Movement acceptance
  • Reload and projectile spawn
  • Hits, armor, and damage
  • Spotting and hidden state
  • Destruction and match result

Private/LAN rooms use browser-hosted WebRTC authority; ranked uses a renderer-free Node service. Current room flow carries host-selected maps, player-selected camouflage, authenticated chat, invitation metadata, non-host rejoin, garage room presence, team/ready state, and round-to-round persistence. Separate browser rigs certify four-player and live 7v7 moving-and-firing combat, including sync, reconciliation, rubber-banding, event delivery, and render behavior.

08 // Performance + GPU

Exceptional performance is an ownership rule.

The garage does not build an unused battlefield. Battle entry performs required warmup before countdown. Hot loops reuse scratch state. Repeated world/tank resources are cached or pooled. Effects have admission budgets. Public presentation routes avoid booting the playable renderer.

BudgetMechanismInvariant
SimulationBounded fixed-step accumulator at 60 Hz.Graphics quality cannot change combat.
RenderingHigh-refresh frame scheduler, adaptive scale, quality tiers, fused post output.Certified runs reached native 120 FPS; that is a measured path, not a promise for every device.
Draw/GPUInstancing/merging, LOD cadence, shadow proxies, cached worlds, resource disposal.Cosmetic cost yields before authority.
LoadingGarage-first boot, lazy battle graph, shader/material warmup, staged world activation.Required battle work finishes before countdown.
Network20 Hz viewer-filtered snapshots, deltas, keyframes, event channel, bounded prediction.Hidden transforms never reach unauthorized viewers.

09 // Mobile compatibility

The same combat rules, a smaller device bill.

Phones and tablets receive dual-stick movement, swipe aim, pinch-to-scope, held/dynamic fire, accessible ammunition and equipment placement, safe-area-aware layout, compact settings, and automatic device tiers. Boot-time GPU probes, pixel-ratio caps, memory-conscious detail, shadow rescue, PMREM validation, and a black-scene watchdog let presentation recover without changing gameplay.

10 // Scene Studio

The shipped renderer is the production stage.

Open /studio or press F8 in the garage. Studio places any roster vehicle on any map, conforms actors to terrain, applies camouflage and damage states, stages game-authentic firing/destruction effects, scrubs a deterministic 20-second timeline, builds camera/actor/effect rails, records video, captures high-resolution PNGs, and round-trips scene JSON. The new shared Field Frames drawer keeps 50 current references beside the authoring controls.

11 // Tank Gallery

Live geometry, searchable and reviewable.

The Tank Gallery searches the sealed fleet registry and constructs the actual procedural vehicle. Hero/front/side/rear/top cameras, hull/turret/gun articulation, armor/module/crew overlays, normalized ratings, ammunition tables, and exact triangle or coplanar-patch markup all read canonical game data. Portable JSON/PNG packets preserve pose, camera, rig path, mesh identity, bounds, centroid, triangle, and requested operation.
