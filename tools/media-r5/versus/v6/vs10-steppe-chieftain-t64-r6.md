# vs10-steppe-chieftain-t64-r6: the night kill shot

**Composition.** A low lens at 0.9 m sits 10 m from the Chieftain Mk 10, which fills the left half of the frame (51 %
of its width) in front three-quarter, 45° off its nose. Its turret is traversed 96° so the barrel runs across the frame
as a leading line onto the T-64BV, and the muzzle is frozen 0.06 s after the killing shot. The T-64BV is 41 m out at
the frame's centre-right, still facing the Chieftain, and is brewing up:
- a penetration's white-hot pop and spall on its front plate;
- then `tank_kill` with an ammunition-rack cause and the turret popping: fireball, ring blowtorch and cook-off pops;
- 40 hot fragments falling;
- an ember storm just behind it.

Behind it on the right stand a cook-off column, a fire field and its embers. A white illumination flare hangs overhead
in the upper middle, 27 m up at 75 m by the still. Together with the blast, it lights the steppe.

**Why it is new.** The old layout had a tank driving in on the left and two tanks trading fire at matching heights.
Here the shooter sits still and the target is dying. The frame is built around the low barrel pointing at a brew-up,
under a flare. The railyard idea is the other night scene, but it is a tracer duel. This one is a single kill: there
are no tracers, and the shot has already landed.

**Lens and light.** Lens a: 0.9 m lift, fov 48°, pitched up 4.5°, looking south-east (bearing 120) over open grass.
Lens b: a 1.2 m, 44° variant. It is night, with the moon at azimuth 260, elevation 34, behind the lens's left, so the
Chieftain's front and right flank catch it. The grade is LIT plus CLEAN at exposure +0.35.

**Check.** Clean, with no `!` lines:

```
lens a  hero 45° off its nose (its right) at 10 m, box x -0.83..0.20 y -0.37..0.56
        foe0 28° off its nose (its left) at 41 m, box x 0.24..0.45 y -0.21..-0.07
lens b  hero 44° at 12 m, box x -0.84..0.14; foe0 29° at 43 m, box x 0.26..0.49
```

There are no wires, and no shrubs or records near the lens or on either sightline. The T-64's ember storm stands 3 m
behind it so that nothing is in front of either hull. The muzzle is at x 0.03, clear of the T-64's box.
